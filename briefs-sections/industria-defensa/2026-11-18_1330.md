## Update: ICEYE–Avio — Brussels applauds, Rome hesitates, and Washington must also approve

New facts since the 20:30 edition of 17 November and the 07:30 edition of 18 November. The bid concerns Avio S.p.A. (Colleferro, Italy: Vega launcher, solid rocket motors), not Avio Aero (GE Aerospace's engine business). Andrius Kubilius, EU Commissioner for Defence and Space, wrote on X that satellite production and launch capability "unites in one company", that this "can have a major impact on the future of 🇪🇺 space activities" and that such consolidation "is exactly what M. Draghi suggested in his famous report". Defence Minister Guido Crosetto told Reuters: "If I were Leonardo, I would think carefully before exiting Avio" (Teleborsa/ANSA). Leonardo holds 18.04% and is 30% owned by Italy's Economy Ministry; without it, ICEYE would need more than 80% of the remaining shares to reach its 66.67% threshold. Government sources told Reuters that Rome had received no notification and that any assessment was "premature". ICEYE's CEO Rafal Modrzewski said he was open to talks, including a possible public partner. Mediobanca Research expects "significant" political and regulatory obstacles and a possible counter-bid by Italian or European investors (MilanoFinanza).

**Why it matters for Europe.** This is an intra-European merger (Finland–Italy) that would join SAR satellites and a European launcher with solid-propulsion know-how that also feeds MBDA. On CEAFE's line that is positive: fewer, larger European champions. Yet the offer notice shows how far US jurisdiction reaches into a deal between two EU firms. Besides Italy's golden power, French and UK FDI clearance and EU foreign-subsidies review, the offer requires approval by the US Committee on Foreign Investment (CFIUS) "without conditions" and a notification to the Pentagon's Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency (DCSA), because of the groups' US operations. No EU authority can block a US merger in the same way. Rome's reluctance is the other risk: a national veto on a European consolidation would cut against the EU-scale industry that Kubilius is calling for.

**Key facts.** €41 per share, up to about €2.05bn, cash; 20% premium; commitments of €1bn of investment over five years and 400 jobs in Italy; combined revenue about €0.8bn (MilanoFinanza). Conditions: 66.67% threshold; Italian golden power; FDI in France and the UK; EU FSR; CFIUS; DCSA notification.

**Sources:** @KubiliusA ([post](https://x.com/i/status/2123004235688403143)), [Iris 2 S.p.A. offer notice (ICEYE, PDF)](https://www.iceye.com/hubfs/102%20Notice%20%2817.11.2026%29%20%5BENG%5D.pdf?hsLang=en), [Teleborsa/ANSA (Crosetto)](https://teleborsa.ansa.it/notiziario/finanza/opa-iceye-su-avio-crosetto-se-fossi-leonardo-rifletterei-prima-di-uscire/), [MarketScreener/Reuters (no notification)](https://it.marketscreener.com/notizie/avio-al-governo-nessuna-notifica-da-iceye-prematura-ogni-valutazione-fonti-ce785edcda8bff21), [MilanoFinanza](https://www.milanofinanza.it/news/avio-iceye-faro-golden-power-2724557), [Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/).

**What to watch.** Leonardo's decision (with the government), Avio's board opinion, the golden-power filing and any counter-bid. Next Vega C (VA-series, IRIDE satellites) is slipping toward Christmas (the 07:30 edition of 16 November).

## Switzerland votes on 29 November on easing its arms-export law: polls at a dead heat

Two final polls published on 18 November show the reform of the Swiss War Materiel Act just ahead but without a majority: 49% yes and 48% no (Tamedia/20 Minuten, LeeWas, 11–12 Nov, >12,000 respondents) and 49% yes, 46% no, 5% undecided (SRG SSR, gfs.bern, 4–11 Nov, 13,527 respondents, ±2.8 points). The law passed by parliament in December 2025 would allow exports to 25 mostly Western countries even when they are in a conflict, while giving the government a veto on neutrality grounds (Bloomberg via swissinfo). Swiss manufacturers say neutrality-based restrictions have pushed them to move capacity abroad; they lost business after Russia's invasion of Ukraine and face a further drop after the Iran war triggered a ban on shipments to the US. The divide is political (FDP 75–79% yes, Greens and SP reject it), by gender (59% of men in favour, 38% of women in Tamedia's poll) and by age.

**Why it matters for Europe.** Swiss firms are part of European supply chains, and Swiss refusals to let European buyers pass on Swiss-made ammunition and equipment have been a recurring problem for European armies since 2022. A "yes" would make Swiss suppliers more predictable for the 25 listed partners. It is also a European parallel to the ITAR debate (the 07:30 edition of 18 November): whoever controls re-export rules controls the use of the product.

**Sources:** [swissinfo (Bloomberg)](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/swiss-face-nail-biter-vote-on-allowing-more-weapon-exports/92397966), [swissinfo (SRG poll)](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/votes/november-29-votes-opposition-to-pension-financing-plan-edging-ahead/92396495), [SRF](https://www.srf.ch/news/zweite-srg-umfrage-kriegsmaterialgesetz-pattsituation-mit-leichtem-ja-vorteil), [20 Minutes](https://www.20min.ch/fr/story/sondage-20-mintamedia-tva-et-initiative-du-centre-rejetes-materiel-de-guerre-incertain-103669075), Bloomberg ([post](https://x.com/i/status/2122958029075128749)).

**What to watch.** Result on 29 November; which 25 countries are on the list; reactions from German and Danish buyers.

## PLD Space signs its first commercial launch: Mitsubishi Electric on MIURA 5's second flight

Spanish launcher start-up PLD Space announced on 18 November its first commercial launch contract: a Mitsubishi Electric demonstration satellite for the Japanese group's planned mega-constellation will fly on the second test flight of MIURA 5. Mitsubishi Electric is also an investor: it put €50m into PLD Space in March 2026, leading the €180m Series C, and led a €108m extension on 1 September, with "priority access" to MIURA 5 launches. No date is set; the maiden flight from the Guiana Space Centre slipped in October to the first half of 2027 (European Spaceflight).

**Why it matters for Europe.** A second European commercial launcher (after Isar's Spectrum, which reached orbit from Andøya) is part of the answer to the launch squeeze left by Vega C delays and the Vulcan failure (the 07:30 edition of 17 November). The anchor customer and lead investor is Japanese, not European. European institutional customers (ESA's European Launcher Challenge, national governments) would give such firms a domestic market.

**Sources:** @PLD_Space ([post](https://x.com/i/status/2122977305106567566)), [European Spaceflight](https://europeanspaceflight.com/pld-space-investor-selects-miura-5-to-launch-demo-mission/), SpaceNews ([post](https://x.com/i/status/2122932907744317744)).

**What to watch.** MIURA 5 qualification and the first-flight date; ESA Launcher Challenge contracts.

## DLR chief: Europe needs a heavy-lift launcher competition and a German sea-launch trial

At Space Tech Expo Europe in Bremen, Walther Pelzer, head of the German Space Agency at DLR, said Europe relies only on Ariane 6 for heavy lift and should extend the European Launcher Challenge model to larger rockets. He said Germany could trial a sea-launch capability next year with a sounding rocket fired from a vessel, an offshore option that could later be placed near the equator. He also said the EU should treat UK and Norwegian launch sites as European, and that Germany is working with Canada and Spain on launch infrastructure. Trying to calm fears that Berlin is going it alone in security space, he said Germany had invited the EU and ESA to join its "Protocol Stack for Space" (European Architecture for Space Interoperability) (Aviation Week).

**Why it matters for Europe.** Independent access to space is a precondition of a European defence capability (reconnaissance, communications, navigation). A heavy-lift competition would avoid relying on one launcher, while the UK/Norway point raises the question of how far "European" preference extends. The interoperability offer responds to the France–Germany friction reported in the 07:30 edition of 18 November.

**Sources:** [Aviation Week](https://aviationweek.com/space/launch-vehicles-propulsion/dlr-boss-wants-european-heavy-lift-rocket-challenge), [DLR](https://www.dlr.de/en/latest/news/2026/space-tech-expo-2026-dlr-showcases-technologies-for-the-future-of-spaceflight), Aviation Week ([post](https://x.com/i/status/2122973838270976034)).

**What to watch.** ESA ministerial decisions on launchers; any German sea-launch test date.

## European space round-up from Bremen: Estonia's Space Cyber Range, Dcubed's €27.2m round, ICEYE processing

**Estonia** is offering a Space Cyber Range to countries and companies for testing satellites, ground stations and operations against cyber attacks, fully operational in 2027 (Minister of Economy and Industry Liina Vahtras, Aviation Week). It is being developed under ESA's ARTES 4S programme by Spaceit, CybExer Technologies, CGI Estonia and the CR14 foundation (a NATO DIANA test centre), with the University of Tartu. Context: Russia's 2022 cyberattack on Viasat. [Aviation Week](https://aviationweek.com/defense/sensors-electronic-warfare/estonia-offers-satellite-cybersecurity-test-range), [Spaceit](https://spaceit.eu/services/space-cyber-range/).

**Dcubed** (Germany; release actuators, deployable structures, solar arrays) closed a €27.2m Series A+ (about $30m) led by Poland's Warsaw Equity Group with the EU's EIC Fund and HTGF. It will quadruple production in Germany and the United States and fly its ARAQYS solar array in 2027; it has 87 staff and more than 500 products in space. An EU-funded company is scaling up partly in the US, which fits CEAFE's watch on EU-funded technology moving to the US. [HTGF](https://www.htgf.de/en/dcubed-raises-oversubscribed-27-2-million-round/), [SpaceNews](https://spacenews.com/dcubed-raises-30-million/).

**ICEYE** says processing of its Spot Fine SAR image has fallen from about 35 to about 6 minutes in a year (@iceye_global, [post](https://x.com/i/status/2122963040786075743)).
