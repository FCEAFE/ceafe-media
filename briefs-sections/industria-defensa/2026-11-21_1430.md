## Auterion and Ukraine's SkyFall plan a drone joint venture producing in Germany

**Auterion and SkyFall announced on 20 November that they intend to form a joint venture, "Skyfall x Auterion", to build drones at high volume in a new factory in Germany, with German funding and a path to supplying the German armed forces.**

The partners say they have already delivered more than 50,000 Shrike strike drones to Ukraine together. Production starts with SkyFall's Shrike FPV airframes running Auterion's avionics and AuterionOS; interceptors and deeper-strike drones may follow (Auterion; Breaking Defense).

**Why it matters for Europe.** European money and a German site, but the autonomy software layer comes from Auterion, which operates from the US. Europe gains production capacity and Ukrainian combat experience. The question is who controls the software and its export licences.

**What to watch.** The JV's ownership split, where the IP sits, and whether the Bundeswehr orders.

## Morocco commits €366m to build a national defence industry by 2035

**Morocco has allocated 4 billion dirhams (about €366 million) from the Hassan II Fund to its "Morocco Offer" defence and security industry roadmap (Royal Office statement; Euronews, 20 Nov).**

By 2035 it aims to create about 50,000 jobs, produce 40% of its military and security equipment at home and earn over 65% of sector revenue from exports. Priorities are drones and autonomous systems, land and naval systems, defence electronics, cyber defence and AI, with industrial zones in Benguerir and Benslimane.

**Why it matters for Europe.** It is a market and a partner on Europe's southern flank. European firms (French, Spanish) compete there with US, Israeli and Turkish suppliers for joint ventures.

**What to watch.** The first foreign industrial partners named for the Benguerir and Benslimane zones.

**Sources:** [maroc.ma](https://maroc.ma/en/news/statement-royal-office-12), [·](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/20/morocco-commits-366-million-to-build-a-national-defence-industry-by-2035)

## USAF opens a $16.1bn propulsion contract to 58 companies, Rolls-Royce and Safran included

**The Air Force named 58 companies on a multiple-award contract for "advanced propulsion enterprise work" with a ceiling of $16.1 billion through 2041 (Breaking Defense, 20 Nov; DoD contracts).**

Awardees include Boeing, GE Aerospace, Honeywell, Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, RTX, Rolls-Royce, Safran, Williams International and Hermeus. The ceiling is not guaranteed spending: only small initial amounts were obligated. The owner reposted the story.

**Why it matters for Europe.** European engine makers get access to the US market through their US subsidiaries, under US control rules (FOCI, ITAR). European firms have no reciprocal multi-billion European propulsion framework.

**What to watch.** Task orders, and whether the European firms' share goes through US subsidiaries.

**Sources:** [breakingdefense.com](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/air-force-issues-massive-16b-contract-for-engine-work-to-dozens-of-companies/)

## EU defence ministers endorse a joint statement on defence security of supply

**At the EDA Steering Board, EU defence ministers endorsed a Joint Statement on strengthening defence security of supply for Defence Readiness 2030 (EDA, 21 Nov).**

The EDA says it helps member states identify strategic dependencies and assess supply-chain weaknesses in the European Defence Technological and Industrial Base.

**Why it matters for Europe.** Security of supply is where dependence on non-European components (chips, raw materials, propulsion) turns into an operational risk. Joint European action is the only scale that works.

**What to watch.** Whether the statement leads to joint stockpiles or binding priority-order rules.

**Sources:** [eda.europa.eu](https://www.eda.europa.eu/what-we-do/all-activities/activities-search/security-of-supply), [·](https://x.com/i/status/2124050186439643403)

## Danish GomSpace wins €8m to build lunar prospecting satellites for Blue Origin

**GomSpace has secured an €8 million phase-two contract to keep developing two spacecraft for a lunar resource-prospecting mission.**

GomSpace called the customer only "a leading North American space company"; European Spaceflight identifies it as Blue Origin from the company's earnings calls. An initial €2.9 million contract was awarded in January 2026.

**Why it matters for Europe.** European satellite know-how is going into a US lunar resource programme. Europe still has no comparable lunar programme of its own to keep it at home. SpaceNews meanwhile reports that Europe's space community is again making the case for a European human spaceflight programme.

**Sources:** [europeanspaceflight.com](https://europeanspaceflight.com/denmarks-gomspace-secures-e8m-contract-for-blue-origin-moon-mission/), [·](https://x.com/i/status/2124065966812184678)
