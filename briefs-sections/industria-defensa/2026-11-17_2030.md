## NATO starts the 11-nation Starlift launch programme, without France or the US

**NATO began implementing Starlift, a multinational project to secure military satellite launches on commercial rockets from spaceports across NATO territory and South Korea.**

Ten allies (Belgium, Canada, Finland, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Norway, Sweden, Turkey, the UK) and South Korea committed funding through the NATO Support and Procurement Agency (NSPA), agreed on the sidelines of the National Armaments Directors' meeting in late October. Four original participants, France, Hungary, Luxembourg and the United States, were not in the announcement; NATO gave no reason and disclosed no costs, according to Breaking Defense.

**Why it matters for Europe.** Responsive launch is a gap Europe has long filled by buying SpaceX rides. Starlift could create demand for European small launchers and spaceports (Andøya in Norway, Spaceport Cornwall in the UK are named by Breaking Defense), but it is a NATO, not an EU, framework and includes South Korea; France, which owns Europe's main spaceport in Kourou, is absent. Read with topic 1, it shows the case for a European launch capability owned and procured by Europeans.

**What to watch.** NSPA tender for launch services; whether French providers can bid.

## Italy orders SAR-L, a national L-band radar satellite, from Thales Alenia Space

**The Italian Space Agency (ASI) signed a contract with Thales Alenia Space (Thales 67%, Leonardo 33%) as prime contractor for SAR-L, a 2.2-tonne L-band synthetic-aperture-radar satellite in sun-synchronous orbit with a deployable antenna of more than 26 m².**

Thales Alenia Space will build the whole system in-house, including the radar electronics and antenna. SAR-L will provide continuity with the L-band data Italy now gets from Argentina's SAOCOM satellites and complement the X-band COSMO-SkyMed constellation and Copernicus, for ground deformation, geological risks, civil protection and "territorial security". No contract value was published.

**Why it matters for Europe.** It is a European-built sovereign Earth-observation asset with security uses, supplied entirely by a Franco-Italian company, and it ends a reliance on a non-European (Argentine) source for L-band data. Italy now has X-band (COSMO-SkyMed), and will have L-band, alongside Copernicus C-band.

**What to watch.** launch date and launcher choice (a Vega C would close the loop with topic 1).

## European exports: Fincantieri's WASS to co-produce torpedoes in India; MBDA weapons for a South African drone; an ITAR-free test bench

**India: Fincantieri's WASS Submarine Systems signed an MoU with state-owned Bharat Dynamics (BDL) in New Delhi to explore Indian production of heavyweight torpedoes and torpedo countermeasures (components, energetic sections, final assembly, testing, in-service support).**

- **India:** Fincantieri's WASS Submarine Systems signed an MoU with state-owned Bharat Dynamics (BDL) in New Delhi to explore Indian production of heavyweight torpedoes and torpedo countermeasures (components, energetic sections, final assembly, testing, in-service support). It follows WASS's contract of more than €200m (Rs 1,896 crore, December 2025) for 48 Black Shark Advanced torpedoes for India's six Scorpène (Kalvari-class) submarines; indiaeuropa reports a plan for over 200 more torpedoes made in India with BDL as partner.
- **South Africa:** MBDA and Milkor signed an MoU (at AAD 2026, Pretoria) to integrate MBDA missiles on the Milkor 380 MALE drone, shown with two Sea Venom/ANL anti-ship missiles (120 kg, 30 kg warhead, up to 30 km). It is a framework, not a contract; integration, firing trials and export approvals remain (Military Africa, defenceWeb).
- **France:** MBDA and the French atomic and alternative energies commission (CEA) presented IRRIS, an infrared-scene test system described as "sovereign and ITAR-free".

**Why it matters for Europe.** European weapons win export markets where US, Turkish and Chinese suppliers compete (Military Africa notes African armed drones come mostly from Türkiye and China), and an ITAR-free product means no US veto on re-export. The India deal also shows how far co-production now goes: European firms are expected to transfer production, as US firms do in Poland (topic 6).

## Poland's Patriot offset: Lockheed Martin hands MESKO rocket-motor design software

**Lockheed Martin and Polish state-owned MESKO (PGZ group) completed the first project of the WISŁA Phase II offset: MESKO received advanced software, training and technical support to design solid rocket motors quickly, including larger-calibre motors.**

It is one of eight Lockheed Martin commitments (plus five by Raytheon, about 1bn złoty in total) attached to Poland's purchase of six more Patriot batteries (48 launchers), LTAMDS radars and up to 644 PAC-3 MSE missiles, according to Portal Obronny and defence24. @F_CEAFE reposted the announcement.

**Why it matters for Europe.** Offsets are the consolation prize of buying American: some know-how stays in Poland, but the system, its source codes, upgrades and export permissions stay in the US. The European alternative existed: the Franco-Italian SAMP/T NG (Eurosam: MBDA and Thales), with Aster interceptors made in Europe. The same day, Germany's minister Pistorius said Germany and Norway will jointly buy a further package of US weapons for Ukraine (repost by @F_CEAFE of @GermanyNATO, https://x.com/i/status/2122658548798660659), another case of European money flowing to US industry. Israel's Elbit, a major supplier to European armies, also reported a record $33bn backlog and 17% revenue growth in Q3 (https://x.com/i/status/2122584225605943500).

**What to watch.** Patriot dependence thread ([2026-11-16_0730.md](2026-11-16_0730.md), [2026-11-16_2030.md](2026-11-16_2030.md)); CARD's long-range interceptor family (topic 2).
