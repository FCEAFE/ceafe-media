## Paris and Berlin split over "made in Europe" in the next EU defence budget

France wants EU defence money in the 2028–2034 budget (European Competitiveness Fund) to finance equipment made in Europe, to cut dependence on foreign — mainly US — suppliers; Germany, itself a buyer of US equipment, wants member states free to choose suppliers, including American ones (BFM Business, 27 Nov). Rapporteur reports that twelve countries oppose the French push for an EU defence preference. BFM frames it as a new Franco-German rift after the end of FCAS. Linked to the MFF row covered on 25–26 Nov (Kubilius' warning of cuts; Tusk: "a cheap Europe could turn out to be a very expensive mistake").

**Background (CEAFE archive):** CEAFE has argued for a European preference since at least November 2019, when @F_CEAFE cited NATO's AGS programme, which bought the US Global Hawk and set aside the European manned platform (https://x.com/i/status/1197129297221345287); in February 2020 it called for flagship European defence programmes "Galileo style", backed by legislation (https://x.com/i/status/1227350335905243141).

**Assessment:** CEAFE's line backs a European preference: EU money spent on non-European systems deepens technological dependence and funds the competitors of Europe's own industry. No member state can sustain a full defence industrial base alone.
Sources: https://www.bfmtv.com/economie/entreprises/defense/la-france-veut-que-l-argent-europeen-finance-des-armes-europeennes-l-allemagne-veut-pouvoir-acheter-americain-paris-et-berlin-se-dechirent-encore-apres-le-fiasco-du-scaf_AN-202611270152.html · https://www.rapporteur.com/news/12-countries-oppose-french-push-for-eu-defence-preference/ · https://x.com/clashreport/status/2125967586881896461 (reposted by @F_CEAFE)

**CEAFE:** Without a common industrial, technological and defence base, Europe will remain dependent on decisions taken in Washington or Beijing.

## After FCAS: CDU's Luftwaffe rapporteur proposes a German–Swedish–Spanish fighter partnership

Volker Mayer-Lay, CDU/CSU rapporteur for the Luftwaffe, argues for developing Germany's next combat aircraft with Sweden (Saab) and Spain, with Canada possibly joining later, and rejects joining GCAP because workshares there are already allocated and Germany would only get a junior role (hartpunkt, 26 Nov; ESUT). Airbus' works council has called for development to start immediately.

**Background (CEAFE archive):** in February 2020, when the FCAS demonstrator phase was launched, @F_CEAFE reposted Airbus's announcement (https://x.com/i/status/1227742993098080258) and argued that Spain had to be in the programme; as early as October 2019 it backed the Eurofighter for Germany as a European option paving the way for FCAS (https://x.com/i/status/1181446737795452929).

**Assessment:** relevant for Spain (Airbus Defence and Space, Indra). A European programme is positive; the risk is further fragmentation into three European fighter efforts (GCAP, a French programme and a German-led one) while the F-35 fills the gap.
Sources: https://www.hartpunkt.de/kampfflugzeugentwicklung-cdu-berichterstatter-fuer-die-luftwaffe-setzt-auf-partnerschaft-mit-schweden-und-spanien/ · https://esut.de/2026/11/meldungen/77101/fcas-cdu-politiker-schweden-gcap/ · https://x.com/hartpunkt/status/2125974224753881139 (reposted by @F_CEAFE)

## Leonardo rolls out the first of 15 M-346s for Italy's Frecce Tricolori

Leonardo presented the first of 15 M-346 aircraft in a dedicated configuration for the Italian Air Force's national aerobatic team (PAN, Frecce Tricolori), with a livery designed by Pininfarina, and flight testing has begun; the M-346 PAN replaces the MB-339. CEO Lorenzo Mariani called it "a major success for Leonardo and for Italy".
**Assessment (positive):** national industry supplying its own air force's flagship team.
Sources: https://x.com/Leonardo_live/status/2126026455528124918 · https://x.com/Leonardo_live/status/2126038487724118123

## Rocket Lab sets an Electron annual record with its 101st launch (Synspective StriX)

Electron "Night Owls Never Sleep" lifted off from Launch Complex 1 (New Zealand) at 00:17 UTC on 27 Nov (01:17 Madrid) and deployed the 14th StriX SAR satellite for Japan's Synspective into a 569 km, 38° LEO; it was Electron's 22nd launch of the year, a new annual record, and its 101st overall.
**Assessment:** a US–New Zealand small launcher serving a Japanese SAR constellation; Europe's small-launcher offer (Vega C, Spectrum, RFA One) is still short of this cadence.
Sources: https://x.com/RocketLab/status/2126108006740021276 · https://x.com/RocketLab/status/2126124723688100180 · https://x.com/RocketLab/status/2126143472382132677
