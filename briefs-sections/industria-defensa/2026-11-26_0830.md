## Kubilius to EU Chiefs of Defence: CARD 2026 shows Europe risks missing its 2030 readiness target

Defence and Space Commissioner Andrius Kubilius posted on 25 November, after his keynote to the EU Military Committee at Chiefs of Defence level, that "Russia continues to escalate", "we aren't producing enough for Ukraine" and that the CARD Report 2026 shows "we risk missing our strategic target of defence readiness by 2030" ([@KubiliusA](https://x.com/KubiliusA/status/2125700387936108785)). The EEAS readout says the Chiefs, chaired by General Seán Clancy, with an opening address by Kaja Kallas and a briefing by EDA Chief Executive André Denk, called for faster air defence for Ukraine before winter and for removing barriers to military movement across the EU by 2027; they welcomed the extension of the EU Military Assistance Mission for Ukraine to November 2028 (EEAS). The same evening Kubilius relayed the European space industry's warning that the proposed 30% cut to the MFF defence and space line "would make it impossible to deliver the EU's space ambitions" ([@KubiliusA](https://x.com/KubiliusA/status/2125655246529802255)).


**CEAFE comment:** When capabilities are compared — economic, technological or military — European unity stops being a slogan and becomes a condition.

**Why it matters for Europe:** the Commission's own review says fragmented national efforts are not enough to reach readiness by 2030; joint procurement and a defence budget at EU scale are the answer, not national silos. The space-cut warning is the industrial side of the same problem.

**What to watch:** the European Council budget talks opening today in Brussels, and the publication of the CARD 2026 findings.

Sources: [EEAS](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/eeas/eu-chiefs-defence-call-faster-delivery-european-defence-readiness-0_en); [@KubiliusA](https://x.com/KubiliusA/status/2125700392642384340); [Commission speech page](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/speech_26_2586) (page returned no text).

## Bulgaria buys five more German IRIS-T SLM systems for about €925.9m with EU SAFE loans

Bulgaria's parliament has ratified an agreement with Germany to buy five more IRIS-T SLM air-defence systems for about €925.9m, funded through the EU's SAFE loan programme, Global Defense Corp reports (its headline says four; the text says five). It is phase two of Bulgaria's IRIS-T programme under the European Sky Shield Initiative, bringing the two phases to about €1.106bn; an amendment to the contract with Diehl Defence is expected. The decision followed drone attacks on two merchant ships in Bulgaria's Black Sea exclusive economic zone. Each battery uses a Hensoldt TRML-4D radar.


**Background (CEAFE archive):** In November 2019 @F_CEAFE noted how close Bulgaria's defence relationship with the US was, leaving "not much room to cooperate in #EUDefence" (https://x.com/i/status/1196064454200512512); in June 2023 it greeted Germany's order for six IRIS-T SLM fire units with #BuyEuropean (https://x.com/i/status/1672224725693521921). A Bulgarian IRIS-T buy financed with EU SAFE loans marks that shift.

**Why it matters for Europe:** a European buyer, a European system, EU money: exactly the intra-European procurement the SAFE instrument was built for, and a counter-example to European purchases of US air defence.

**What to watch:** the Diehl contract amendment and the planned IRIS-T SLX buy by 2032.

Sources: [Global Defense Corp](https://www.globaldefensecorp.com/2026/11/26/bulgaria-purchases-four-more-iris-t-slm-missile-defence-systems-using-eu-backed-funds/).

## State Department approves a $621.9m F-16 support package for Ukraine, paid by Belgium, Denmark, the Netherlands and Norway

The US State Department approved on 25 November a possible sale to Ukraine of F-16 maintenance support and related equipment worth about $621.9m, including aircraft pyrotechnic cartridges, laboratory equipment and transport support, The New Voice of Ukraine reports. Belgium, Denmark, the Netherlands and Norway will pay, with no US funds; contractors will be selected competitively. President Zelensky said in October that about half of Ukraine's F-16s were in Europe for maintenance, and on 17 November that Ukraine needs 40 more F-16s.

**Why it matters for Europe:** European taxpayers are financing a US support contract for a US jet. Europe already maintains these aircraft (Belgium's Sabena Aerospace Engineering is pictured in the report); the more of this work goes to European MRO firms, the more of the money stays in Europe. In the longer run Gripen and Rafale, not more F-16s, are the European fighter answer for Ukraine.

**What to watch:** the formal notice on the State Department site and which European firms win the work.

Sources: [The New Voice of Ukraine](https://english.nv.ua/nation/state-department-approves-622m-f-16-sale-to-ukraine-50662644.html).

## FCC authorises SpaceX to deploy up to one million "Starmind" data-centre satellites, and weighs reciprocity against foreign operators

The FCC granted SpaceX authority for its "Starmind" orbital data-centre system: up to one million low-Earth-orbit satellites with a 15-year licence. Deployment must begin by 25 November 2033, with 100,000 satellites by 2035, 500,000 by 2038 and the full million by 2040; primary links are optical, with Ka-band only for backup telemetry, tracking and command (FCC order, Spaceflight Now, NBC). Separately, the FCC said it will consider reciprocal restrictions on foreign satellite operators, a post @F_CEAFE reposted ([@FCC via @F_CEAFE](https://x.com/F_CEAFE/status/2125720675419828569)). SpaceNews also carried the authorisation ([@SpaceNews_Inc](https://x.com/SpaceNews_Inc/status/2125662971418366315)).

**Why it matters for Europe:** a licence for a constellation of this size would claim orbital slots and spectrum on a scale no European operator can match, and the reciprocity threat could be aimed at operators such as Europe's in the US market just as the EU defends its 2 GHz band and IRIS². Orbital and spectrum sovereignty is now industrial policy.

**What to watch:** the FCC reciprocity proceeding and the EU's reply on IRIS² and 2 GHz (deal expected 1 December).

Sources: [FCC order (PDF)](https://eu-assets.contentstack.com/v3/assets/blt23eb5bbc4124baa6/bltf4cdf2af47159e12/6b071c5f5506bb38bca5b0ec/FCC_report_and_order_on_SpaceX_Starmind_AI_constellation_11-25-2026.pdf); [Spaceflight Now](https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/11/26/fcc-green-lights-spacexs-plan-to-launch-1-million-starmind-data-center-satellites/); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/science/space/spacex-fcc-authorizes-1-million-satellites-data-centers-space-rcna611341).

## Senate appropriators move to bar sole-source Space Force contracts above $100m that went to SpaceX; White House seeks $5bn for Golden Dome in the stopgap

A bipartisan group of senators is moving to limit SpaceX from winning non-competitive Space Force missile-defence contracts, Bloomberg reports ([@business](https://x.com/business/status/2125658367158788487)). According to search results citing the bill, Section 8127 of the Senate FY2027 defence bill bars the Space Force from using funds for sole-source contracts above $100m for the Space Data Network and space-based AMTI, both first awarded to SpaceX; the bill funds Golden Dome at $9.39bn. Meanwhile the White House OMB has asked Congress for a $5bn anomaly in the continuing resolution to keep Golden Dome going, and for help to replenish the MQ-9 fleet, ahead of the 11 December funding deadline (Air & Space Forces Magazine; [@ASForcesMag](https://x.com/ASForcesMag/status/2125707466939773171)).

**Why it matters for Europe:** Washington is actively avoiding dependence on a single space supplier (see the $2.29bn SDN Backbone award of 22 November) while Europe still relies on Starlink in Ukraine. The lesson for the EU is the same: competition and sovereign alternatives (IRIS²) before lock-in.

**What to watch:** whether Section 8127 survives conference, and the CR text before 11 December.

Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-25/spacex-sole-source-deals-targeted-in-defense-spending-bill) (unreachable, timed out); [Air & Space Forces Magazine](https://www.airandspaceforces.com/pentagon-congress-help-with-golden-dome-mq-9/); [@AviationWeek](https://x.com/AviationWeek/status/2125724213663154195).

## India's 76 naval utility helicopters (about Rs 15,000 crore) put Leonardo and Airbus in front

The Indian Navy (51) and Coast Guard (25) are preparing to buy 76 Naval Utility Helicopters to replace the Chetak, in a programme estimated at about Rs 15,000 crore, likely under Buy and Make (Indian), which requires foreign makers to team up with Indian firms, the Times of India reports. European manufacturers are expected to be front-runners: Leonardo's AW169M (Leonardo partnered with Adani Defence and Aerospace in February 2026) and Airbus, with HAL also in focus.

**Why it matters for Europe:** a large European export opportunity in the Indo-Pacific, and an example of the cooperation with India that widens Europe's strategic options beyond US-led blocs.

**What to watch:** the request for proposals and the Indian partners chosen.

Sources: [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/defence/news/navy-coast-guard-helicopter-acquisition-puts-leonardo-airbus-and-hal-in-focus/articleshow/136152110.cms); [@timesofindia](https://x.com/timesofindia/status/2125733775770284163).

## Update: Poland seeks concrete UK and French air-defence help as CAMM-MR moves ahead

Polish Defence Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz is asking the UK and France to turn their security commitments into concrete air-defence, missile-defence and reconnaissance support, and Warsaw plans a no-fly zone along its eastern border by late December or early January (Anadolu, PAP; [@anadoluagency](https://x.com/anadoluagency/status/2125669599593025729)). Aviation Week adds that Poland wants to expand its commitment to MBDA's CAMM family with the medium-range version developed with the UK, following the intergovernmental agreement of 24 November covered on 24–25 November ([@AviationWeek](https://x.com/AviationWeek/status/2125707359603351651); Janes). Cross-reference: see World/Conflicts for the no-fly zone.

**Why it matters for Europe:** Poland turning to European partners and a European missile (PGZ–MBDA UK) is the positive trend; the test is whether London and Paris deliver.

Sources: [PAP](https://www.pap.pl/en/news/poland-introduce-no-fly-zone-east-amid-russian-missile-threat); [Aviation Week](https://aviationweek.com/defense/missile-defense-weapons/poland-cements-camm-mr-missile-interest-uk-handshake-agreement); [Janes](https://www.janes.com/defence-intelligence-insights/defence-news/sea/poland-and-uk-advance-camm-mr-missile-project-three-years-after-concept-unveiled).
