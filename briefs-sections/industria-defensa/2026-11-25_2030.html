Update: Hera reveals a stadium-sized crater on Dimorphos, the asteroid humanity moved

ESA said on 25 November that its Hera mission, arriving at the Didymos–Dimorphos binary, found that the 151-metre moonlet Dimorphos now has one face flattened by a stadium-sized crater left by NASA’s DART impact of 26 September 2022, is tumbling chaotically, and that hundreds of previously unknown boulders encircle the pair (ESA). DART had shortened Dimorphos’s orbit by 33 minutes, far more than predicted; Hera was sent to measure what the impact actually did. The probe’s onboard computer (ADPMS-3) is supplied by US firm Redwire, through its Belgian unit (Redwire).

Background (CEAFE archive): @F_CEAFE reposted ESA’s Hera launch on a SpaceX Falcon 9 (7 Oct. 2024) and, on 17 Nov. 2026, ESA’s update that Hera was studying Didymos from 28.5 km away.

Why it matters for Europe: planetary defence is one of the few space fields where Europe leads a mission end to end. It shows what ESA can deliver when member states pool funding, the opposite of the budget cuts Commissioner Kubilius warned against at 14:30.

What to watch: the full Hera science results and any follow-up ESA planetary-defence mission (Ramses/Apophis) in the ESA budget.

Sources: ESA; @esa; SpaceNews; @Redwire.

Israel adds about $5bn to its 2026 defence budget to buy “blue-and-white” national systems

Israel’s Joint Committee on the Defence Budget approved an addition of about NIS 17.3bn (about $5bn) to the 2026 defence budget, Breaking Defense reports. Defence Minister Israel Katz said it will allow “blue-and-white self-reliance in long-term procurement in space, attack helicopters, air defense, and other classified areas”. The 2026 budget was approved in March at a record $45bn and has since received several supplements (August, September, and an October deal to fund Apache purchases).

CEAFE comment: Without a common industrial, technological and defence base, Europe will remain dependent on decisions taken in Washington or Beijing.

Why it matters for Europe: a country of fewer than 10 million people is spending to cut its dependence on foreign suppliers, while many European states still buy off-the-shelf from the US. European self-reliance needs the same political will, but it only works at the EU scale, since no member state is big enough on its own.

What to watch: Israeli contracts for national space and air-defence systems, and Israeli exports to Europe (Arrow 3, SkyStriker).

Sources: Breaking Defense (via @BreakingDefense).

Embraer and ALADA sign a framework to sell the KC-390 and A-29 government-to-government in Latin America and Africa

Embraer and ALADA (Empresa de Projetos Aeroespaciais do Brasil) signed a framework agreement at Mostra BID in Brasília on 25 November. It opens the way to offer the KC-390 Millennium transport and the A-29 Super Tucano to countries that buy through government-to-government (G2G) models, starting with Latin America and Africa. It follows a memorandum signed at FIDAE in April 2026 (Embraer).

Background (CEAFE archive): The KC-390 is a constant in the CEAFE archive as a European purchase: Portugal’s deliveries (2024) and sixth aircraft (June 2025), and Colombia’s selection (5 Aug. 2026).

Why it matters for Europe: a G2G channel backed by the Brazilian state makes the KC-390 more competitive against the European A400M and C295 in third markets. Several EU states have already chosen the KC-390 over the A400M. Europe needs a similar joint export-support tool to back its own aircraft.

What to watch: the first G2G contracts under the framework, and KC-390 campaigns in Europe.

Sources: Embraer; @embraer.

US Army declares its first Dark Eagle hypersonic battery fully fielded

Lt. Gen. Frank Lozano, the Army’s acquisition executive for fires, told Breaking Defense on 19 November that the first Long Range Hypersonic Weapon (Dark Eagle) battery “is fully fielded. It is ready to go”. He did not say when or where it was fielded. The programme first aimed to field the battery in 2023 and then missed a September 2025 target. Bloomberg reported that it was still not completely fielded as of October 2026.

Background (CEAFE archive): @F_CEAFE has followed Dark Eagle since an aborted test at Cape Canaveral (Sep. 2023) and CENTCOM’s request for its deployment (30 Apr. 2026).

Why it matters for Europe: the US now has a land-based hypersonic strike capability, and Europe has no operational equivalent. European hypersonic work, such as the EU-funded interceptor studies, is still in early stages. That gap leaves European long-range strike dependent on US decisions.

What to watch: whether Dark Eagle is deployed to Europe, and how ELSA (European Long-Range Strike Approach) progresses.

Sources: Breaking Defense (via @BreakingDefense).

Navantia’s BAC combat supply ship for the Spanish Navy passes its critical design review

Navantia announced that the BAC (Buque de Aprovisionamiento en Combate) programme for the Armada has passed its Critical Design Review. The new ship will add protection, digitalisation and the ability to operate uncrewed vehicles (Navantia).

Why it matters for Europe: naval logistics ships built at home keep shipbuilding capacity in Europe. Navantia has already built replenishment ships for export to Australia.

What to watch: the start of construction, and Navantia’s Industry Day in Chile on 1 December (Exponaval).

Sources: @NavantiaOficial.
