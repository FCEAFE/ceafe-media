## Update: ICEYE's bid for Avio: Rome wants safeguards on solid rocket motors

**Italy wants any takeover of Avio S.p.A. by Finland's ICEYE to keep the company partly Italian, preserve national sovereignty over solid rocket motors and keep Leonardo (18%) as a key shareholder, Reuters reports.**

**Buyer:** ICEYE (Finland), through its Italian vehicle Iris 2. **Target:** Avio S.p.A. (Colleferro, Italy), maker of the Vega C launcher and solid rocket motors, not GE Aerospace's Avio Aero. **Value:** about €2.05bn, all cash at €41 a share (a 21.3% premium), with a plan to delist from Milan and invest a further €1bn in Italy over five years (European Spaceflight; ICEYE). Defence Minister Guido Crosetto has urged caution, while Leonardo CEO Lorenzo Mariani welcomed the bid. The formal Golden Power review has not yet started.

*CEAFE:* If Europe wants to sit as an equal, it has to pool what is today fragmented.

**Context:** Antecedentes: on 17 November @F_CEAFE reposted ICEYE's announcement of the tender offer "to create a combined European sovereign space champion" ([X, 2026](https://x.com/i/status/2122611622774747212)). ICEYE presents the deal as a way to reduce Europe's reliance on providers such as SpaceX's Starshield.

**Why it matters for Europe:** a European buyer, a European target and a stated aim of a sovereign group able to build, launch and exploit satellites. Rome's concern is that solid rocket motors also feed missiles. The case tests whether national Golden Power rules let European champions form while US groups scale up.

**What to watch:** the Avio board's opinion; the Golden Power notification and conditions; Leonardo's stake; the French position (Vega C is operated by Arianespace for ESA).

## EDF-funded EUROGUARD semi-autonomous vessel launched in Estonia

**The EUROGUARD vessel, a 45-metre semi-autonomous ship, was launched on 18 November in Nasva, Estonia, the European Commission announced on Monday.**

The project, started in 2022, brings together 29 organisations from 10 European countries and is coordinated by the Estonian shipyard Baltic Workboats. The European Defence Fund (EDF) contributes €65m of a total budget of about €95m. The ship can operate with or without a crew; its missions include maritime surveillance, mine detection and clearance, search and rescue, and the protection of undersea cables and energy infrastructure.

*CEAFE:* Europe has resources; what it lacks, time and again, is the scale that only joint action can provide.

**Context:** Estonia is an EU member state and a NATO member; the Baltic has seen repeated undersea-cable incidents.

**Why it matters for Europe:** a joint programme financed from the EU budget for an urgent Baltic need: ten countries and one budget achieve what no small coastal state could fund alone. It is the kind of intra-European procurement CEAFE considers positive.

**What to watch:** sea trials; whether EU member states order production units (EDIP/SAFE); links with NATO's Baltic Sentry.

**Sources:** [European Commission (DG DEFIS)](https://defence-industry-space.ec.europa.eu/edf-funded-euroguard-vessel-launched-estonia-2026-11-23_en)

## Thales Alenia Space wins more than €240m for the Sentinel-3NG Topography altimeters

**Thales Alenia Space, the joint venture of Thales (67%) and Leonardo (33%), has signed a contract worth more than €240m with OHB System AG, the prime contractor, for the two altimeters on the first Copernicus Sentinel-3 Next Generation Topography satellite.**

They are a nadir SAR altimeter for continuity and a new wide-swath interferometric altimeter that will give near-complete ocean coverage in five days (Thales Alenia Space). OHB had earlier won the €554m prime contract for the satellite (OHB).

**Context:** Copernicus is the EU's Earth-observation programme, implemented with ESA.

**Why it matters for Europe:** an all-European value chain (German prime, Franco-Italian payload) financed by the EU and ESA. Copernicus data is dual-use (maritime awareness, Arctic ice) and keeps Europe from depending on US commercial imagery.

**What to watch:** the Sentinel-3NG Optical contract; the choice of launcher.
