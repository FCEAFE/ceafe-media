## France orders 50,000 155 mm shells from KNDS and triples a production site

**The DGA has ordered 50,000 155 mm shells from KNDS, with deliveries planned for 2029 and 2030.**

The order comes with a joint state–KNDS France investment to triple the capacity of the La Chapelle-Saint-Ursin (Cher) site through three new production units (KNDS, 20 Nov). The DGA says French output has risen from 30,000 to 60,000 shells a year since 2022 and will gradually increase to 150,000 a year by 2029 (DGA on X, 20 Nov).

**Why it matters for Europe.** This is European demand going to a Franco-German group. Sovereign ammunition supply is the clearest lesson of the Ukraine war. The scale only adds up if European orders are pooled, so that each national line does not stay subscale next to US production.

**Sources:** [press release, 20 Nov, via](https://knds.com), [press release, 20 Nov, via](https://urlr.me/4GPYvx)

## BAE Systems opens the UK's first fully automated large-calibre ammunition factory

**BAE Systems says it has opened the UK's first fully automated large-calibre ammunition factory at Glascoed, South Wales.**

It says the £180m investment in robotics and autonomous vehicles enables a 16-fold increase in 155 mm shell capacity for the UK and NATO allies (BAE Systems on X, 20 Nov; the press release link was unreachable).

**Why it matters for Europe.** Together with the French order, this shows European capacity being rebuilt at home rather than bought abroad. The UK is outside the EU, so whether it can take part in SAFE-funded demand is the test of a wider European munitions base.

**Sources:** [baes.co](https://baes.co/NRVc5108qlh)

## Two German one-way attack drones pass live-fire tests; first deliveries this year

**Two German one-way attack drone variants passed live-fire trials this month, according to Germany's military procurement office.**

This clears the way for initial deliveries and troop training before the end of the year (Defense News, 20 Nov; headline and summary only).

**Why it matters for Europe.** Loitering munitions from German suppliers instead of US systems such as Switchblade. It is a national step, and the gain comes if these programmes open to other European buyers.

**Sources:** [defensenews.com](https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/)

## Sweden's Nordic Air Defence launches the K200 interceptor against Geran-3a jet drones

**Swedish start-up Nordic Air Defence unveiled the K200 autonomous drone interceptor, successor to the Kreuger 100XR, designed to intercept jet-powered one-way drones including Russia's Geran-3a.**

The firm has opened a staffed office in Ukraine, where testing will shape further development (Breaking Defense, 20 Nov).

**Why it matters for Europe.** Cheap European interceptors are the answer to firing expensive missiles at cheap drones, and testing in Ukraine speeds up the learning cycle. This comes on the day Poland reported that two Russian missiles and a drone violated its airspace overnight (Reuters, 20 Nov).

**Sources:** [breakingdefense.com](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/swedens-nordic-air-defence-launches-k200-drone-interceptor-pushes-toward-ukraine-tests/)

## Update: Baykar begins Kizilelma deliveries to the Turkish military

**Baykar has begun delivering its jet-powered Kizilelma drones to the Turkish military, letting the NATO member start using unmanned aircraft in air-to-air combat (Bloomberg, 20 Nov; Baykar on X, "from the first design to the first delivery").**

This follows the 19 Nov 20:30 item on Kizilelma entering service.

**Why it matters for Europe.** Turkey fields a combat drone ahead of any European unmanned fighter. Baykar already owns Piaggio Aerospace in Italy, so it is also a player inside the European industrial base.

## France's third Suffren-class SSN, De Grasse, enters active service

**The French Navy's chief, Admiral Cluzel, declared the nuclear attack submarine De Grasse, built by Naval Group, admitted to active service on 20 November (Marine Nationale, reposted by Naval Group).**

**Why it matters for Europe.** It shows a fully sovereign European nuclear submarine capability. Only France has one in the EU, and the UK has the other in Europe.
