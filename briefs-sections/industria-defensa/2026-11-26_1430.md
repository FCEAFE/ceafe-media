## Update: Europe pays, the US sells — $621.9m F-16 sustainment for Ukraine funded by Belgium, Denmark, the Netherlands and Norway

The State Department has approved a possible Foreign Military Sale to Ukraine of F-16 sustainment worth an estimated $621.9m: cartridge and propellant-actuated devices, laboratory equipment and transportation support, as an addendum to an earlier F-16 support case (Interfax-Ukraine, AeroTime, ArmyInform). The notice states that Ukraine will use JUMPSTART (Joint Ukraine Multinational Program – Services, Training and Articles Rapid Timeline) funding from Belgium, Denmark, the Netherlands and Norway, and that "there will be no U.S. funds associated" with the purchase. @F_CEAFE quoted exactly that passage this morning ([@F_CEAFE](https://x.com/F_CEAFE/status/2125899036146151706)). New since 08:30: the JUMPSTART mechanism and the addendum nature of the case are now confirmed by several outlets quoting the official notice.


**CEAFE comment:** Acting together is not a political ornament: it is the only way to reach a scale that others already have.

**Why it matters for Europe:** four European states pay, and the money flows to a US programme for a US aircraft. Europe already does much of the hands-on F-16 maintenance (e.g. Belgium's Sabena Aerospace Engineering); the European alternatives for Ukraine's air force in the long run are Gripen, Rafale and — see item 5 — the Eurofighter.

**What to watch:** the contractors chosen and how much of the work goes to European MRO firms.

Sources: [@F_CEAFE](https://x.com/F_CEAFE/status/2125899036146151706); [Interfax-Ukraine](https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/general/1242600.html); [AeroTime](https://www.aerotime.aero/articles/us-ukraine-f-16-sustainment-sale-european-allies); [ArmyInform](https://armyinform.com.ua/en/2026/11/26/us-approves-621-9-million-package-for-ukrainian-f-16s-who-will-pay-for-fighter-jet-maintenance/). ## "Black Sands": first joint US–Israel counter-drone trial in Israel, about 20 systems including the Iron Beam laser

Israel's Ministry of Defense announced on 26 November that, "for the first time in Israel and with 20 participating systems", Israel and the US held a joint trial of advanced interception solutions against drone threats ([@Israel_MOD](https://x.com/Israel_MOD/status/2125897042367287669), reposted by [@F_CEAFE](https://x.com/F_CEAFE/status/2125906657456906275)). The campaign, named Black Sands, was led by IMOD's Directorate of Defense Research & Development (DDR&D) with US bodies including the Capabilities Development and Innovation office (CD&I), Special Operations Command Central (SOCCENT), CENTCOM, the Office of Naval Research and JIATF 401 (Calibre Defence, Israel Hayom). The press release names no systems; Calibre Defence identifies in the official video Rafael's **Iron Beam** high-energy laser (in IDF service since its development finished in September 2025), Rada **ieMHR** radars, the US **Allen Control Systems Bullfrog M240** autonomous weapon station and Smart Shooter's **SMASH Hopper**. Targets ranged from large fixed-wing drones to small quadcopters.


**Background (CEAFE archive):** CEAFE has followed Iron Beam since March 2022, when Israel's Ministry of Defence announced funding for its development and production by Rafael ([repost](https://x.com/i/status/1504887092886786058)); in April 2022 Rafael reported a successful laser intercept. Rafael announced delivery of the system to the IDF on 28 December 2025 ([repost](https://x.com/i/status/2005326280284332116)), and in March 2026 Israel reported its first use against Hezbollah rockets ([repost](https://x.com/i/status/2028402002300961152)).

**Why it matters for Europe:** lasers and automated guns are becoming the cheap answer to mass drones. Europe has its own programmes (UK DragonFire, Rheinmetall/MBDA lasers, HENSOLDT Elysion — item 6) but no comparable joint, multi-vendor field test at this scale; the US and Israel are pooling test data that Europe does not see.

**What to watch:** which systems IMOD and the US select for accelerated procurement.

Sources: [@Israel_MOD](https://x.com/Israel_MOD/status/2125897042367287669); [Calibre Defence](https://www.calibredefence.co.uk/black-sands-israel-and-us-trial-counter-drone-systems-following-bruising-encounters-in-recent-wars/); [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.com/2026/11/26/israel-us-test-advanced-drone-interception-systems/); [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/defense-and-tech/article-916186).

## M&A: Helsing (Germany) reported to be weighing a takeover of Destinus (Netherlands)

Handelsblatt reported, citing four people familiar with the matter, that Germany's Helsing is considering buying the Netherlands-based cruise-missile and drone maker Destinus (Hengelo). It would be the largest deal in Helsing's history; experts cited by Handelsblatt put Destinus' enterprise value at $3–6bn. Helsing told Reuters it is "not currently involved in a takeover process"; Destinus declined to comment. Helsing took part in Destinus' latest funding round (Reuters via Euronext; reposted by [@F_CEAFE](https://x.com/F_CEAFE/status/2125906881181016325)).


**Background (CEAFE archive):** CEAFE has tracked Helsing since February 2025, when it announced 6,000 HX-2 strike drones for Ukraine ([quote](https://x.com/i/status/1890130654840439121)); in June 2025 it bought Grob Aircraft, in February 2026 it teamed up with Hensoldt on autonomous combat air systems, and on 4 November 2026 it handed Germany ownership of its combat-system software (Reuters, [post](https://x.com/i/status/2118140243778138503)).

**Why it matters for Europe:** buyer Helsing (Germany), target Destinus (Netherlands), value not disclosed. Unlike the recent US takeovers of European suppliers (Steyr Motors, Truflo Marine, Tekne), this would keep a long-range strike maker in European hands — consolidation of the kind that builds European scale. Status: report, denied by the buyer; not a confirmed deal.

**What to watch:** any formal approach, and Dutch investment screening.

Sources: [Reuters via Euronext](https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/germanys-helsing-considering-takeover-destinus-handelsblatt-says); [Handelsblatt](https://www.handelsblatt.com/unternehmen/industrie/marschflugkoerper-helsing-prueft-laut-insidern-kauf-von-destinus/100271588.html) (paywall); [NOS](https://nos.nl/regio/overijssel/artikel/822806-duitse-krant-meldt-mogelijke-overname-van-wapenfabrikant-destinus-in-hengelo).

## UK signs £8.7bn "Gateway Agreement" with Babcock for submarine support — largest contract in Babcock's history

Babcock and the UK Ministry of Defence signed the Gateway Agreement, a five-year framework worth £8.7bn (potential aggregate term up to 15 years) succeeding the FMSP contract, effective 1 December 2026. It covers submarine maintenance, engineering, technical and naval-base services at HMNB Devonport, Devonport Dockyard and HMNB Clyde, including the transition from Vanguard to Dreadnought class. Babcock says it safeguards 8,500 jobs and adds 3,000 new roles. The MOD calls it an £8bn investment in submarine availability ([@DefenceHQ](https://x.com/DefenceHQ/status/2125896340110533043)).

**Why it matters for Europe:** a sovereign, national-industry support contract for the nuclear deterrent; availability of the UK's attack submarines has been a known weakness.

Sources: [Babcock](https://www.babcockinternational.com/news/babcock-signs-record-8-7-billion-gateway-agreement-to-strengthen-the-uk-submarine-enterprise/); [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/major-8-billion-investment-to-upgrade-uk-nuclear-submarine-availability-and-warfighting-readiness); [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/politics/2026/nov/26/ministry-of-defence-deal-babcock-maintain-submarines).

## Ukraine formally seeks 10–20 of Italy's retiring Tranche 1 Eurofighters

Ukraine has formally asked Italy for 10–20 of its first-generation Eurofighter Typhoons; Italy has 26 Tranche 1 jets in service, to be retired gradually by 2029 and replaced by 24 new Eurofighters. Rome and Kyiv are negotiating, but no quantity, date or approval is set; a transfer would need Italian parliamentary authorisation and the consent of the other Eurofighter partners (Army Recognition, defence-blog; reposted by [@F_CEAFE](https://x.com/F_CEAFE/status/2125896030835134702)).

**Why it matters for Europe:** a European fighter for Ukraine instead of more US airframes — the contrast with item 1. It also needs the four-nation Eurofighter consortium (Germany, UK, Italy, Spain) to agree.

Sources: [Army Recognition](https://www.armyrecognition.com/news/aerospace-news/2026/italy-considers-transfer-of-eurofighter-typhoon-fighter-jets-to-ukraine-amid-fleet-renewal); [defence-blog](https://defence-blog.com/ukraine-formally-seeks-italys-retiring-eurofighters-report-says/); [la Repubblica](https://www.repubblica.it/esteri/2026/11/12/news/giorgetti_firma_decreto_aiuti_ucraina_kiev_chiede_a_italia_eurofighter-425716846/).

## HENSOLDT and ARGUS Interception expand counter-drone partnership

HENSOLDT will integrate ARGUS Interception's net-based, non-kinetic A1-Falke interceptor into its Elysion C-UAS platform, linking detection, C2 and controlled capture of hostile drones where kinetic means are unsuitable; first joint demonstrations and customer projects are planned. Billed as drone defence "Made in Germany" for critical infrastructure and military sites.

Sources: [HENSOLDT](https://www.hensoldt.net/news/hensoldt-and-argus-interception-are-expanding-their-partnership-for-effective-drone-defence); [hartpunkt](https://www.hartpunkt.de/hensoldt-und-argus-interception-bauen-partnerschaft-fuer-drohnenabwehr-aus/).

## EDA study: EU maritime transport capacity for military mobility

A new European Defence Agency study assessed 141 ports in 22 EU countries; access to ports and ships varies and some terminals are nearing capacity. EDA will work with member states on the gaps ([EDA factsheet](https://eda.europa.eu/docs/default-source/applicants/factsheet---study-on-maritime-capacity-20261116.pdf)).
