Italy orders a third LSS logistic support ship: Fincantieri–Leonardo via OCCAR (~€500m)

The temporary grouping (RTI) led by Fincantieri with Leonardo signed an amendment to its OCCAR contract for the LSS programme on 26 Nov, at Muggiano, covering a third logistic support unit for the Italian Navy. Reported value about €500m including €88m in options (Fincantieri ~€414m, Leonardo ~€85m). Assessment (positive): European yard and systems house, procured through OCCAR, Europe’s joint procurement agency — the model of intra-European procurement the editorial line favours. Sources: https://x.com/Fincantieri/status/2126011073484472829 · https://www.fincantieri.com/it/newsroom/news-e-comunicati-stampa/2026/fincantieri-nuovo-contratto-per-la-costruzione-della-terza-unit-lss-per-la-marina-militare · https://www.bairdmaritime.com/security/naval/naval-auxiliary-support/italian-navy-logistic-support-ship

Update: Tusk: “a cheap Europe could turn out to be a very expensive mistake”; Kubilius at NATO JSEC before the final Military Mobility trilogue

Polish PM Donald Tusk posted “a cheap Europe could turn out to be a very expensive mistake. Security comes at a price.” Defence and Space Commissioner Andrius Kubilius replied “Cannot agree more!”. Kubilius also spent the day at NATO’s JSEC HQ in Ulm (Germany) ahead of the final EU Military Mobility trilogue, warning that “EU & NATO can’t afford asynchrony”. Linked to the MFF row (25 Nov 1430: Kubilius warned of a ~€39bn cut to defence and space). Assessment: the budget fight is now explicit at head-of-government level; no single member state can fund this alone. Sources: https://x.com/KubiliusA/status/2125990273519952123 · https://twitter.com/donaldtusk/status/2125984390484226064 · https://x.com/KubiliusA/status/2125989475402633678

Thales Alenia Space to set up a dedicated company (SPV) for its ISS cargo-return service

According to European Spaceflight (26 Nov), citing a 13 Nov statement by Italian unions FIM, FIOM and UILM, Thales Alenia Space expects ESA to finish evaluating its ALADDIN proposal by end-November; if selected it would bring in private investors and create an SPV to develop, finance and commercialise its cargo spacecraft (work since ESA’s May 2024 LEO Cargo Return contracts, with The Exploration Company). Watch: who the private investors are — an ESA-funded capability should stay under European control. Source: https://europeanspaceflight.com/thales-alenia-space-to-establish-dedicated-company-for-cargo-return-services/ (via https://x.com/AndrewParsonson/status/2125976300506788213)

Denmark ratifies its security agreement with the US on Greenland

Defense News reports Denmark’s parliament unanimously ratified the security agreement with the United States on Greenland on Thursday, hoping to end the standoff over the island’s future. Assessment: an EU member state accepting terms under US pressure over its own territory — a sovereignty and economic-coercion story to follow. Source: https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/ (headline and summary; full article not fetched)

France and Sweden hold their first joint nuclear-deterrence exercise

Per Defense News, the ministries said the exercise involved no actual nuclear weapons and targeted no country. Assessment (positive): the French deterrent’s European dimension is taking practical form with a non-NATO-nuclear partner. Source: https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/ (headline and summary)

Embraer and the Brazilian Army complete development of the SABER M200 Vigilante radar (RoW)

Announced at Mostra BID (Brasília, 26 Nov): the R&D cycle run by the Army Technological Center (CTEx) is complete after exercises TÍNIA, SAGITTA PRIMUS and PERSEU; the second serial-configuration unit is in production. Brazil keeps building a sovereign air-defence sensor base. Source: https://www.embraer.com/media-center/en/?mediatype=NEWS&detail=34112-Embraer-and-Brazilian-Army-Conclude-Development-and-Operational-Validation-of-the-SABER-M200-Vigilante-Radar

Breton on Nvidia and US chip export controls: “rapport de force”

Former Commissioner Thierry Breton says US media and lawmakers speak of a scandal over possible circumvention of US chip export bans to China, and that Europe must stop talking about competitiveness and “assume the balance of power”. Context for the extraterritorial export-controls thread (US rules bind Europe, while the US bends them at home). Claims in his posts are his; not independently verified.
