## Update: Benghazi raid — Zelensky admits no coordination with Europeans; Rome says "case closed, alarm not"

In a call with Giorgia Meloni on Friday, Volodymyr Zelensky confirmed that Ukraine's 22 November naval-drone operation against Russian vessels in the Libyan port of Benghazi "was carried out without prior coordination with partners". He undertook to ensure "full information sharing and direct, constant communication" (Palazzo Chigi). Meloni told him the operation "violated Libya's sovereignty and risks extending the conflict to the Mediterranean", and that Italy's support "cannot translate into the acceptance of initiatives that put the security of Italy and the region at risk". The call followed a BBC interview in which Zelensky had said Kyiv "never" acts without coordinating with allies. Defence minister Guido Crosetto said that "none" of the EU, NATO, France, Germany or the UK had known (Il Sole 24 Ore). Foreign minister Antonio Tajani said: "The government has never lied… Zelensky's apology makes everything definitively clear." He added that if Rome had been told in advance it would have objected: "violating Libya's sovereignty is a mistake and does not help Ukraine's cause". He asked all parties to refrain from further military action in Libyan territory and waters. Italy has military personnel, a navy ship and the Guardia di Finanza in Libya, as well as energy terminals supplying Italy.

**CEAFE angle (owner's posts):** @F_CEAFE reposted Zelensky's statement on the call and Tajani's "there was never any coordination with the allies" clip (Clash Report).

**Why it matters for Europe:** Ukraine took a unilateral step on the EU's southern flank, in a country where Italy has forces and energy interests. It shows how little say Europeans have over the escalation of a war they finance. Clash Report also says a Russian convoy carrying military vehicles to Libya turned back in the Mediterranean after the sinking (not verified). Russia's "Libyan Express" logistics are a southern-flank issue for NATO and the EU.

**Key facts:**
- 22 Nov: SBU says the corvette *Merkury* (Project 20380) and a Ka-27 helicopter were sunk, and the cargo ship *Angara* hit (Interfax-Ukraine).
- Libya's Government of National Unity (Tripoli) condemned the attack and asked Kyiv for an explanation.
- 27 Nov: the Meloni–Zelensky call; 28 Nov: Tajani's interview with Quotidiano Nazionale (republished by the Farnesina).

## Russian missiles over Poland: Canada summons Russia's ambassador; Moscow rejects the charge

Global Affairs Canada summoned the Russian ambassador in Ottawa on Friday to condemn "Russian missile strikes in Poland, the latest example of Russia's reckless aggression, which heightens the risk of unintended escalation". It added: "Canada stands side by side with its Allies. We are fully committed to the defence of NATO territory and to supporting Ukraine." Polish prosecutors say they identified debris from three Russian Kh-101 cruise missiles that entered Polish airspace during the 20 November attack on Ukraine, found at Nowy Lipowiec (Lublin) and at Rudnik and Molodycz (Subcarpathia), and manufactured in Dubna in October or November (Anadolu). Poland's MFA summoned Russia's ambassador-designate on 24 November. **Russia's response:** Ambassador Oleg Stepanov said Russia rejects accusations of violating EU and NATO airspace. He said Moscow "is not interested in escalation or direct military confrontation with NATO" but will ensure its security "with all seriousness and resolve", and that the West made accusations "without convincing evidence" (Lenta.ru, Russian media). Russia's embassy in Warsaw had called the charges "baseless" (RBC).

**Why it matters for Europe:** a NATO ally across the Atlantic is acting diplomatically over EU airspace, while Poland's request to intercept Russian missiles over Ukraine has found "no determination" among allies (see 26 Nov). The eastern flank's air defence remains the clearest case for common European capabilities.

## Update: Gulf — France condemns Iranian attacks on Kuwait and Bahrain; Saudi alerts; drones at RAF Lakenheath

France "condemns in the strongest terms the Iranian attacks on the Kingdom of Bahrain and the State of Kuwait in recent days, which killed two people of Italian nationality and injured several others". It calls them "unacceptable and unjustifiable" and urges "the utmost restraint" (France Diplomatie, 27 Nov). Pakistan's prime minister Shehbaz Sharif condemned "the recent drone attack on Kuwait". Bahrain had invoked the GCC principle that "any attack on one member state constitutes an attack on all" and UN Security Council Resolution 2817 (2026) (BNA, Bahraini state agency). On Saturday, Saudi Civil Defense issued several threat alerts for Jazan, Khamis Mushait and Abha (Anadolu), after the Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted a Houthi ballistic missile. CENTCOM rejected an Iranian commander's claims that the IRGC navy retook two tankers seized by the US: "Iran no longer has a navy." @F_CEAFE reposted an OSINT report that a UAE government-linked jet (A6-RJC) landed in Tehran on Saturday morning (purpose unknown; not confirmed officially). In the UK, US forces repelled about six drones near RAF Lakenheath on 13 November, with Iran suspected, according to the FT (via Reuters and Clash Report).

**Why it matters for Europe:** Europeans are among the dead in the Gulf war, and European bases are reportedly being probed. Yet EU policy appears mainly as national statements (France, Italy) rather than a common position. Former Qatari PM Hamad bin Jassim's call — "We genuinely need a NATO… joint air defence… technology that we develop together" — applies to Europe as well. Military detail is in Conflicts.

## Asia-Pacific: Taiwan's local elections, PLA pressure, Manila vs Beijing, Japan weighs nuclear submarines

Taiwan held local elections on Saturday. The KMT held Taipei, where Mayor Wayne Chiang fended off the DPP, and claimed Kinmen (Quemoy). The DPP retained Kaohsiung and the KMT lost the Changhua county seat (SCMP, Alibaba-owned Hong Kong daily). Taiwan's defence ministry detected 33 PLA aircraft sorties, 27 of them crossing the median line, plus 8 PLAN vessels and 4 official ships around the island up to 06:00 local time (reposted by @F_CEAFE). In Manila, coast-guard spokesman Jay Tarriela backed defence secretary Gilberto Teodoro: "China has no moral basis to lecture the Philippines on whom we should work with" (reposted by @F_CEAFE). Japan is expected to say in its year-end security-document revisions that it will "begin considering and reach a conclusion" on acquiring nuclear-powered submarines (Japan Times). Global Times (Chinese state media) reports that the J-35A stealth fighter has arrived in Zhuhai for its first static display (reposted by @F_CEAFE). South Korea's NIS is monitoring a possible visit to China by Kim Jong Un.

**Why it matters for Europe:** the Taiwan Strait and the South China Sea are where the US asks Europeans to send warships. The results of the Taiwanese vote and Japan's nuclear-submarine debate (AUKUS-style dependence on US technology) will shape those requests.
