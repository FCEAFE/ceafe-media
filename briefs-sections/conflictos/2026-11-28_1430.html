Update: US–Iran blockade: mines, a disputed strike and Trump’s timing

  • Facts: CENTCOM says it disabled Pacific Archer with a precision strike on its stern on 26 Nov (@sentdefender https://x.com/i/status/2126514662971724034). The IRGC says two tankers hit mines near Khasab and in the eastern Strait (Al Jazeera https://x.com/i/status/2126442190276591784). Iran’s Navy commander claims CENTCOM charges $2.5 million per ship escorted through the southern corridor, with no refund if the ship is hit (@MenchOsint https://x.com/i/status/2126420765377482900); this is an Iranian claim. The IMO Secretary-General, Arsenio Dominguez, told Al Jazeera the IMO has no information on any Iran–Oman agreement to regulate traffic in the Strait (https://x.com/i/status/2126567092963209320). Trump says victory over Iran will come “whenever I want it” but he is giving the regime “a chance to be smart” (@FaytuksNetwork https://x.com/i/status/2126378283633684836; primary video not checked).
  • Europe: Italian FM Antonio Tajani says, citing Kuwaiti investigators, that an Iranian-made drone killed two Italians in Kuwait; Tehran denies launching it from its territory and offers a joint investigation (Al Jazeera https://x.com/i/status/2126472453232717999).
  • Why it matters: European nationals and shipping are already paying the cost of the blockade war.

Update: Saudi Arabia–Yemen war escalates

  • Facts: Saudi-led coalition spokesperson Maj. Gen. Turki al-Maliki says a Houthi cruise missile aimed at Riyadh was intercepted (Al Jazeera https://x.com/i/status/2126523001458078176). Ballistic missiles were fired at Jizan and Khamis Mushait; the Saudi spokesperson says one was intercepted (@MenchOsint https://x.com/i/status/2126610162332872753). Yemen’s internationally recognised government forces launched air strikes across Houthi-held territory, with at least 121 Houthis killed or injured in a day (Al Jazeera https://x.com/i/status/2126629825943310408). Houthi-affiliated Al Masirah says Saudi strikes hit telecom networks in al-Bayda (Al Jazeera https://x.com/i/status/2126525237491593607). Debris in Al-Jawf is reportedly from a Chinese-made DF-15A, Saudi Arabia’s second known launch; Riyadh has never acknowledged owning it (@ClashReport https://x.com/i/status/2126609543555961147; @MenchOsint https://x.com/i/status/2126434287780044823).
  • Claims: Houthi-aligned accounts say a Saudi air strike killed 11 civilians, mostly children, in Al-Turbah, Taiz (via @MenchOsint https://x.com/i/status/2126402116076114361); not independently verified.

Ukraine war: the Kerch bridge and deep strikes

  • Facts: The Telegraph reports Zelensky wants the Crimean (Kerch) bridge destroyed completely and Ukraine is preparing more strikes, after the 18 Nov attack destroyed part of the road crossing (@ClashReport https://x.com/i/status/2126605412124496164, https://x.com/i/status/2126604385593577489). The UK Ministry of Defence published satellite imagery of the bridge and its air defences (via @MilitarySummary https://x.com/i/status/2126377715301548098; UK MoD original not checked). A pro-Russian account cites the Wall Street Journal as saying Ukraine used Israel’s HiSKY satellite communications in the attack (https://x.com/i/status/2126640229201367310); WSJ original not checked.
  • Deep strikes (claims): the Black Spark group says it struck, with Ukraine’s Special Operations Forces, the Efremov synthetic rubber plant in Tula region on the night of 27 Nov, describing it as important for the missile programme (via @Schizointel https://x.com/i/status/2126610107806953818). Russia’s MoD says it intercepted 1,601 drones in 24 hours (https://x.com/i/status/2126610121014853657).
  • Black Sea: Russian drones hit two grain carriers near Odesa, Virtuous Striker and Gladiator, both Greek-managed and bound for Egypt and Turkey; no injuries (via @Schizointel https://x.com/i/status/2126377868729147876).
  • Leaders: Zelensky says the US “can’t be just a neutral mediator” and questions US reluctance over bases in Ukraine (@ClashReport https://x.com/i/status/2126585059201188033, https://x.com/i/status/2126588219567272326). Poland’s FM Sikorski compares Russia’s strikes on Ukraine to Nazi V-2 attacks on London (https://x.com/i/status/2126413340180365735).
