## Update: Iran war — Tehran says tankers hit mines in Hormuz and demands "coordinated" passage

Iran's navy says a tanker hit a naval mine east of the Strait of Hormuz and caught fire, after allegedly ignoring warnings and switching off its transponder (Al Jazeera English, https://x.com/i/status/2125607765234106736). Reuters reports Iran saying ships hit mines and warning vessels to coordinate their passage (https://x.com/i/status/2125620778058055951). The IRGC Navy spoke of two "rogue" tankers on fire and released drone footage (https://x.com/i/status/2125568864217936305). US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent claims Iran has not loaded a single oil tanker since Washington launched "Operation Economic Outcast" in August (Al Jazeera English, https://x.com/i/status/2125608823326945706). In Washington, CNN links October's inflation rise partly to the Iran war (via @atrupar, https://x.com/i/status/2125625301686276125).


**Background (CEAFE archive):** Mines in Hormuz are a recurring thread in the CEAFE archive: reports of Iran laying mines in the US-backed southern Omani corridor (27 Sep. 2026) and the IRGC Navy's claim that two supertankers caught fire after hitting mines in the southern strait (Al Jazeera, 19 Nov. 2026).

**Why it matters for Europe:** a declared mine threat in Hormuz affects European energy supply and shipping insurance directly; Europe has no naval mine-countermeasure presence of its own there under a common flag.

## Finland and Norway's back channel to Putin over the Baltic

Finnish President Alexander Stubb and Norwegian Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Støre reportedly sought to avert a military confrontation with Russia in the Baltic Sea, first using a Russian oligarch as an intermediary and then speaking directly with Putin on 27 October (@ClashReport citing a press report, https://x.com/i/status/2125651487149748343; original report not yet checked). Western intelligence officials say Moscow has reinforced Kaliningrad with drones, powerful radars and electronic-warfare equipment (relayed by @shashj, https://x.com/i/status/2125637622244442519), who also criticises allied signalling on a peacetime blockade (https://x.com/i/status/2125638552679588038).

## Ukraine: first laser interception of a Russian jet-powered drone; port-blockade cost

Ukraine shot down a Russian jet-powered Shahed-type drone with a laser air-defence system for the first time, according to presidential adviser Serhiy "Flash" Beskrestnov (@ClashReport, https://x.com/i/status/2125619210600477164; Kyiv Independent, https://x.com/i/status/2125648797166624798). Fog and cloud over Kyiv had so far limited the system. A study cited by Kyiv Independent estimates a year-long blockade of Ukraine's three main Black Sea ports could cost $9.8 billion in 2027 (https://x.com/i/status/2125638345422508222). The EU backs Ukraine against the IAEA handing a lead role to a Russian (https://x.com/i/status/2125620853916230014), and Ukraine's parliament approved joining the management committee of the special tribunal on Russian aggression (https://x.com/i/status/2125620616292147242).
