## Update: Iran widens attacks to Kuwait and Bahrain as US strikes Qeshm

**Iran's army said it launched drones at US drone and missile installations at Kuwait's Ali al-Salem Air Base, according to IRNA, after Kuwait reported its air defences engaging hostile drones.**

Bahrain's Defence Force accused Iran of ongoing attacks and said air defences intercepted several Iranian aerial attacks before an all-clear. An OSINT timeline (@FaytuksNetwork) lists Iranian attacks on Bahrain at 07:00Z, a US strike on Qeshm around 13:00Z, Iran on Kuwait at 21:40Z and on Bahrain again at 23:10Z on 17 November. A US official described the Qeshm strikes as a response to Iranian aggression; Iranian media say they caused no infrastructure damage. US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said Iran poses no “imminent threat” to the US and dismissed a report of a possible dirty-bomb plot; Iran's government, via IRNA, called the “dirty bomb” story a fabricated pretext for aggression.

**Why it matters for Europe.** The war around Hormuz keeps tanker traffic and energy prices hostage, and Europe is again a bystander: the escalation is managed between Washington and Tehran, with the EU absent from both the fighting and the bargaining.

**What to watch.** Kuwaiti and US damage reports from Ali al-Salem; whether Iran keeps striking several Gulf states a day; any Hormuz item at the Trump–Xi–Putin meeting in Shenzhen.

## Update: Destinus (Netherlands) says its Ruta missiles hit the Kerch bridge

**Destinus, a Dutch-headquartered start-up, said Ukraine fired nine of its Ruta Block 1 cruise missiles at the Crimean (Kerch) Bridge and that seven hit it.**

Its president, Oleksandr Danyliuk, told Reuters they were the only foreign-made weapons used in the strike, alongside the company's Da Vinci (“Lord”) deep-strike drones; Ukraine's FP-5 Flamingo missiles by Fire Point were also used (@sentdefender, video of three Flamingo launches). New footage shows the main road span collapsing after a missile impact (@OSINTtechnical). OSINT accounts also report the destruction of the Mius River road bridge on the R-280 highway, which cuts the safer coastal route between Rostov and the occupied south, and list most road and rail links into Crimea as cut or damaged, with ferries still running (@Schizointel).

**Why it matters for Europe.** It is a rare operational role for a European-made long-range weapon in this war: a system designed and produced in Europe (Destinus' newsroom presents Ruta B1 as “the production system behind 1,750 missiles”), not dependent on US export permission. CEAFE's line on the war remains a negotiated, pan-European peace with Ukraine, Russia and the EU at the table; the strike also raises the risk of Russian retaliation against European firms.

**What to watch.** Russian MoD/MFA reaction naming Destinus or the Netherlands (none found on their Telegram channels at time of writing); repairs and ferry capacity to Crimea.

## Pentagon criticises Ukraine's deep strikes, and Germany and the UK push back

**At the Ukraine Defense Contact Group in NATO headquarters on 17 November, US Under Secretary of War for Policy Elbridge Colby said Ukraine's deep strikes “have not been effective in driving an end to the war” nor in blunting Russia's advances, and warned that strikes on commercial targets such as Wildberries warehouses could stiffen Russian resolve (Reuters, via Kyiv Independent).**

German Defence Minister Boris Pistorius called that criticism “quite a stupid thing”, and UK Defence Secretary Wes Streeting said the strikes had weakened Russian capabilities, bought time and saved lives. Colby said Western aid should prioritise front-line defence and medium-range strike. Separately, the Kyiv Independent reports a draft under which the European Commission would propose an extra €45 billion in loans to Ukraine in 2027.

**Why it matters for Europe.** The US has stopped directly funding new weapons for Ukraine, leaving Europeans and Canada to pay, including for American-made systems, while Washington now tells Kyiv how to fight. Europe carries the cost without setting the strategy, a clear case for the EU acting as one — and, in CEAFE's view, for putting that weight behind a European-led peace process rather than open-ended escalation.

**What to watch.** Commission proposal text; Ukraine talks scheduled for 19 November and the European negotiating group (France, Germany, Italy, Poland, UK, EU and NATO officials, with Kallas and the Baltic states left out, per Euronews).

**Sources:** [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/germany-uk-push-back-after-us-criticizes-ukraines-deep-strikes-into-russia/), [Kyiv Independent](https://x.com/i/status/2122872453303349675), [Euronews](https://x.com/i/status/2122847868117717192), [Swedish government via @camille_grand](https://x.com/i/status/2122826657010680137), [Anadolu](https://x.com/i/status/2122853560677220411)

## LPG carrier GAS HUSKY sinks in Bulgaria's EEZ after drone attack

**The Liberian-flagged LPG carrier GAS HUSKY (IMO 9507764), managed by Greece-based Stealth Maritime, was struck around 23:45 local time on 16 November in Bulgaria's Exclusive Economic Zone while carrying LPG from Alexandria (Egypt) to Midia (Romania).**

“We believe four drones hit us,” the master said, adding the bridge and engine room were hit. All 19 Filipino crew were rescued by a Bulgarian Air Force Cougar helicopter after an Su-25 spotted their life raft; two had minor injuries. BTA reports the tanker later sank, and AP says it is the second merchant ship to sink off Bulgaria's coast in two days.

**Why it matters for Europe.** Attacks are now sinking ships inside an EU and NATO member's EEZ on routes to Romanian ports. Maritime.bg reports the ship vanished from radar around midnight but the emergency beacon fired only at 08:02, and Varna's governor asked why nothing was done for eight hours, which exposes gaps in Black Sea surveillance and rescue.

**What to watch.** Bulgarian investigation and attribution; NATO/EU maritime response in the western Black Sea; insurance on Romanian-bound cargoes.

**Sources:** [Maritime.bg](https://www.maritime.bg/158410/), [BTA](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/bulgaria/1248291-lpg-tanker-gas-husky-sinks-near-bulgarian-coast-all-19-crew-members-rescued), [AP](https://apnews.com/article/bulgaria-romania-black-sea-gas-tanker-gas-husky-3265b6f9e84eb28b349dceb3223fd37f), [Euronews](https://x.com/i/status/2122858066777649261), [@sentdefender](https://x.com/i/status/2122759781354213756)

## Taiwan seeks a 300-satellite constellation, citing Starlink throttling in Ukraine

**Taiwan's space agency TASA is in early talks with “like-minded partners” on a constellation of more than 300 communications satellites, citing the throttling of SpaceX's Starlink service in Ukraine (Aviation Week).**

Ukrainian reporting relayed by OSINT accounts says restrictions on Starshield terminals have continued for days, and The Guardian and The Times say Ukraine bypassed them for the Kerch strike with drone relays.

**Why it matters for Europe.** It shows the kill-switch risk of relying on a single US private provider for military communications, the argument for Europe's own IRIS² constellation and sovereign satellite communications.

**What to watch.** Which partners join; Europe's IRIS² timeline.

**Sources:** [Aviation Week](https://aviationweek.com/space/satellites/citing-starlink-problems-taiwan-pushes-300-sat-multinational-network), [Aviation Week](https://x.com/i/status/2122789122473336843), [@FaytuksNetwork](https://x.com/i/status/2122858362006294721)
