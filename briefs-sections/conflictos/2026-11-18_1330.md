## Update: Hormuz shipping attacks mount as Gulf states condemn Iran's strikes on Kuwait and Bahrain

New since the 07:30 edition: ADNOC said one of its vessels was attacked crossing the Strait of Hormuz, injuring 11 crew with one missing, and UKMTO logged a delayed report of a tanker hit by a projectile 13 nm north of Khasab. Kuwait, the GCC Secretary-General, Qatar and the UAE condemned Iran's drone attacks, while Iran's army chief warned of a response “without geographical restrictions” and Tehran claimed it shot down a US MQ-9. Iran's foreign minister Abbas Araghchi dismissed CNN and New York Times reports of an Iranian “dirty bomb” plot against Washington as “rubbish” (IRNA); France 24 reports Trump said the reports are being taken seriously, while Rubio earlier said he saw no imminent threat to the capital (Reuters).

**Why it matters for Europe.** European energy prices and shipping insurance depend on a strait where, according to Atlantic Council analyst Elisabeth Braw, US naval escorts cannot protect every vessel. Europe has no naval or diplomatic role of its own in the escalation; Qatar, not the EU, is the mediator Tehran cites.

**Key facts.** 11 injured, 1 missing on the ADNOC ship (Al Jazeera); incident 13 nm N of Khasab (UKMTO via Tasnim); Gulf bourses mostly fell (Reuters, https://x.com/i/status/2122983198401482978); a US court sentenced Pakistani businessman Asif Merchant to more than 27 years over a plot he said was directed by Iran's Revolutionary Guard (Anadolu); Qatar's PM said diplomatic efforts for an Iran–US agreement continue (IRNA). The US Navy chief was criticised at a hearing over the Iran war and its toll on sailors (Reuters headline; article returned 401). An IISS Strategic Dossier argues Hormuz cannot easily be reopened by military means against Iran's asymmetric threats (@IISS_org).

**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://x.com/i/status/2122954637150236892) ([2](https://x.com/i/status/2122950475049812314), [3](https://x.com/i/status/2122970567481659743), [4](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/11/18/iran-war-live-kuwait-says-intercepting-hostile-drone-attacks)), [Reuters](https://x.com/i/status/2122929041288372363) ([2](https://x.com/i/status/2122937895962984821), [3](https://x.com/i/status/2122921491587010836)), [France 24](https://x.com/i/status/2122993594604650782), [Anadolu](https://x.com/i/status/2122978367372984748), [IRNA (Iranian state agency)](https://t.me/s/Irna_en), [Tasnim (Iranian state agency)](https://t.me/s/Tasnimnews_en), [@IISS_org](https://x.com/i/status/2122961521739259966)

## Poland reportedly foiled a Russian false-flag plot on the Kaliningrad border

Polish daily Rzeczpospolita, citing senior security sources, reports that Russian intelligence planned a sabotage operation on Polish territory near Kaliningrad in late October or early November, possibly a border crossing and a staged attack, to be blamed on Ukraine with mass casualties. One source compared it to the 1939 Gleiwitz provocation; the stated aim was to halt aid to Kyiv. Warnings came from allied services and Polish analysts, and were also passed to Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia. Tomasz Siemoniak, Poland's minister coordinating the special services, confirmed extra measures “because we considered the threat real” but would not confirm details and cautioned that undisclosed assessments are not established facts.

**Why it matters for Europe.** A staged incident on an EU and NATO border is the kind of provocation that could drag Europe into the war. It also shows that Poland and the Baltic states relied on allied intelligence warnings: a case for pooled European intelligence and border protection rather than 27 separate systems.

**Key facts.** About 180 police from prevention units, counter-terrorism teams, extra Border Guard and Internal Security Agency officers, and GROM, NIL and AGAT special forces deployed to Warmia-Masuria (Rzeczpospolita via Hromadske, Ukrainska Pravda, Polsat). On 3 November Prime Minister Donald Tusk reported “suspicious movement” on the Kaliningrad border (Meduza). No evidence has been published by Polish authorities; list members @mazzenilsson and @GeromanAT called the report unproven.

**Sources:** [Hromadske](https://hromadske.ua/en/europa/274576-poland-thwarted-suspected-russian-false-flag-plot-to-blame-ukraine-for-deadly-attack-report-says), [Meduza](https://meduza.io/en/news/2026/11/18/russia-planned-false-flag-operation-with-mass-casualties-on-polish-territory-rzeczpospolita-reports), [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/11/18/8063407/), [Onet](https://wiadomosci.onet.pl/kraj/rosyjska-prowokacja-na-granicy-z-polska-dziennikarze-ujawniaja-kulisy-operacji/j448987), [Polsat News](https://www.polsatnews.pl/wiadomosc/2026-11-18/rosja-chciala-przeprowadzic-dywersje-w-polsce-miala-przypominac-prowokacje-gliwicka/), [@InsiderGeo](https://x.com/i/status/2122980716698869763), [@mazzenilsson](https://x.com/i/status/2122997817312440792)

**What to watch.** Any official Polish or Baltic statement with evidence; Russian MFA reaction (none on its Telegram channel at time of writing).

## Russia strikes central Odesa and claims hits on Izmail port and a cargo ship

Russia repeatedly struck central Odesa on 18 November, killing 4 and injuring 9 according to the Kyiv Independent's updated count. Ukraine's Air Force said Russia launched 141 long-range attack drones overnight, 73 of them jet-powered, and Russian attacks killed 5 and injured 15 across Ukraine over the past day, including a strike on a market in Zlatopil (Kharkiv Oblast) that injured at least 11. Russia's Defence Ministry said its drones hit port facilities in Izmail used for military cargo and, at sea, a dry-cargo ship it says carried dual-use cargo to the port of Yuzhny (Russian state source, claim).

**Why it matters for Europe.** Izmail sits on the Danube opposite Romania, and the Odesa ports are Ukraine's link to EU markets; with GAS HUSKY sunk in Bulgaria's EEZ (07:30 edition), the maritime war is moving closer to EU waters. CEAFE's line: these are reasons for a pan-European negotiated peace involving Ukraine, Russia and the EU, not for further escalation.

**Key facts.** 141 drones incl. 73 jet-powered (Ukrainian Air Force via Kyiv Independent); 4 killed, 9 injured in Odesa; Zlatopil hit for the second time in seven days. Russian MoD: Izmail port infrastructure and a dry-cargo ship bound for Yuzhny hit (name not given).

**Sources:** [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/russian-strikes-central-odesa-kills-3-injures-7/) ([2](https://kyivindependent.com/russian-attacks-kill-5-injure-15-across-ukraine-over-past-day-as-russia-continues-energy-assault/), [3](https://x.com/i/status/2122992024999592181)), [Russian Ministry of Defence (Russian state source)](https://t.me/s/mod_russia_en)

**What to watch.** Identity and flag of the ship Russia says it hit; Romanian reaction to strikes on Izmail.

## Germany backs Ukrainian findings of Russian chemical weapons use and seeks accountability at the OPCW

Germany's Foreign Ministry accused Russia of “grave breaches of the Chemical Weapons Convention” and said Moscow must be held accountable at the OPCW. A 76-page Ukrainian investigation, carried out with OPCW technical assistance, concludes that a drone dropped an RG-Vo grenade containing CS gas into a Ukrainian trench near Illinka on 20 September 2024, and that this was part of an organised system of manufacture, supply and use. Ukraine says it has documented 14,009 cases; Russia denies using chemical weapons and accuses Ukraine of doing so.

**Why it matters for Europe.** Chemical-weapons attribution is one of the few legal tools Europe can use collectively; the OPCW's annual conference next week gives the EU a chance to speak with one voice.

**Key facts.** Incident 20 September 2024 near Illinka (Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant area); OPCW circulated the report to States Parties this week; New Zealand also said it was “deeply concerned” (RNZ).

**Sources:** [Anadolu (Turkish state agency)](https://aa.com.tr/en/europe/germany-accuses-russia-of-using-prohibited-chemicals-against-ukraine/4128823) ([2](https://x.com/i/status/2123000780739273167)), [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2026/11/18/ukraine-Russia-chemical-weapons/8141794983599/), [RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/politics/2660780/deeply-concerned-peters-reacts-to-russia-s-chemical-weapons-attacks)

**What to watch.** Attribution vote at the OPCW conference; Russian MFA response.

## Update: Houthis fire a cruise missile at Riyadh and threaten Aden

New since the 07:30 edition: the Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted and destroyed a Houthi cruise missile launched toward the Riyadh region (coalition spokesman Turki al-Maliki via Anadolu). Reuters reports Houthi forces are threatening Aden after capturing territory along Yemen's Red Sea coast, raising concerns for the Saudi-backed government and for shipping through the Bab el-Mandeb Strait. Al Jazeera details how the Houthis raise funds for the war through rallies and donations of cash and weapons.

**Why it matters for Europe.** With Hormuz contested, the Red Sea and Bab el-Mandeb are Europe's other energy and trade artery; Houthi control of more of the coast puts the EU's naval operation Aspides and Suez traffic at greater risk.

## Ukraine's drones hit Krasnodar refineries as Russia reports record interceptions

Two oil refineries in Krasnodar Krai's Seversky district, Ilsky and Afipsky, were reportedly targeted in an overnight Ukrainian drone attack on 17–18 November (Kyiv Independent). Russia's Defence Ministry said it intercepted 184 Ukrainian fixed-wing drones overnight over Belgorod, Rostov, Krasnodar, Crimea and the Sea of Azov, and 310 over the past day; Krasnodar officials reported four people injured and fires at two industrial sites (TASS). Ukrainian MP Fyodor Venislavsky said, citing Ukrainian intelligence, that Russia is testing a Geran-6 drone with speeds of up to 1,000 km/h (via @MilitarySummary).

**Why it matters for Europe.** The tit-for-tat energy war keeps oil prices volatile and raises the risk of escalation. CEAFE's line: report the strikes neutrally and push for a pan-European ceasefire framework rather than more long-range weapons.

**Key facts.** 184 drones claimed overnight and 310 over 24 hours (Russian MoD, state source); refineries repeatedly targeted before (Kyiv Independent). Reuters describes Ukraine's “Operation Vivaldi” combining ground robots, drones and jamming to repel Russian assaults.

**Sources:** [Kyiv Independent](https://x.com/i/status/2122917678767824980), [TASS (Russian state agency)](https://t.me/s/tassagency_en), [@MilitarySummary via @GeromanAT](https://x.com/i/status/2122971269188448396), [Reuters](https://x.com/i/status/2122959240381759927)

**What to watch.** Damage at Ilsky/Afipsky (satellite imagery); any confirmation of a Geran-6 in combat.
