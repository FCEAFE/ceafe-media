Update: Strait of Hormuz — tankers on fire, Iranian mine claims, Israeli threat

Iran’s IRGC Navy says two tankers, including a very large crude carrier, caught fire near Khasab early on 26 November, and UKMTO reports a separate projectile strike on an outbound tanker in the eastern Strait on 25 November. Reuters reports Iran claims mine strikes on ships; oil prices first rose on supply concerns, then fell back by midday. An Israeli military spokesperson told Al Jazeera that Israel is ready to attack Iran “with force at any given moment – even independently without the Americans”, while Iran’s nuclear chief Mohammad Eslami called claims that Iran seeks a nuclear weapon fabricated. Al Jazeera, citing local media, says Iran-backed groups in Iraq are preparing to strike US military assets if Washington launches new attacks on Iran.

Background (CEAFE archive): CEAFE has logged Iran’s mining of the Strait since 10 March 2026, when CNN reported the first mines being laid (repost); in April the Pentagon estimated six months to clear them, and on 19 November the IRGC Navy already claimed two supertankers had caught fire after hitting mines (repost).

  • Why it matters for Europe: Hormuz carries a large share of Europe’s oil and LNG imports; every incident feeds energy prices and the EU’s maritime security posture (Aspides).

Update: Saudi–Houthi war — Riyadh alerts and strikes on Sana’a

After the overnight Houthi missile fire reported at 08:30, Riyadh’s airspace was disrupted at 08:30 UTC by missile/drone threats and Saudi airstrikes were reported in Sana’a. New satellite imagery shared by @SoarAtlas shows a large plume of black smoke over the Ras Tanura oil facilities (https://x.com/i/status/2125863393001111789, image not verified). OSINT trackers list regional support flights (Egyptian, Kuwaiti, Turkish and US aircraft) around the Saudi operations.

  • Why it matters for Europe: Ras Tanura is one of the world’s key oil terminals; a widening Saudi–Yemen war threatens Red Sea and Gulf shipping.
  • Sources: https://x.com/i/status/2125873981085397162, https://x.com/i/status/2125915623012049388, https://x.com/i/status/2125863393001111789.

Ukraine: ballistic strikes on Kyiv, warning of a new energy offensive, Eurofighter request

Russian attacks killed 10 people and injured 58 across Ukraine over the past day, with ballistic missiles striking Kyiv and 113 drones launched (Kyiv Independent). President Zelensky says Russia is preparing a new mass attack on Ukraine’s energy infrastructure. Ukraine has reportedly asked Italy for 10 to 20 first-generation (Tranche 1) Eurofighter Typhoons; Italy has 26 Tranche 1 jets in service (@clashreport). Italy has condemned Ukrainian attacks on Russian ships in Libyan waters, warning Kyiv against widening the war (@clashreport, repost). Finnish President Stubb said he does not expect the war to end by the end of the year and that Ukraine must prepare for a harsh winter.

  • Why it matters for Europe: a European fighter transfer would be a European-sourced air-defence contribution; strikes on energy raise Ukraine’s winter needs ahead of EU financing decisions.

Russia’s strategic forces: Sarmat, a general’s arrest, Sputnik-S

IISS analysis of satellite imagery suggests the RS-28 Sarmat ICBM may have begun to enter service with the Strategic Rocket Forces, with further flight trials possible because of reliability concerns. A Moscow garrison military court placed Colonel General Yuri Grekhov, deputy commander of the Aerospace Forces for armaments, in custody on 25 November; charges are not public and reports point to corruption. French analyst Etienne Marcuz discusses the credibility of Russia’s “Sputnik-S” programme, suspected of being an orbital nuclear weapon after a German intelligence warning (counter-space thread).
