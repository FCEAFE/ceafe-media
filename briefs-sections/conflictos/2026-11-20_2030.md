## Iran: CBS says US and Israel weigh a new assault; Tasnim claims US strike on Qeshm; Trump rejects Tehran's posture

****Summary:** CBS News reported that Israel and the US are considering a new assault on Iran in the short term, with Netanyahu favouring a joint operation and Trump undecided (https://x.com/i/status/2123779266731241612; https://x.com/i/status/2123760164574781688).**

Iranian state agency Tasnim said the US struck the outskirts of Qeshm Island with a missile; the Deputy Governor of Hormozgan later attributed the explosion to the destruction of unexploded US-origin munitions (state sources; see Early warning). Trump said Iran “wants a deal that is not acceptable to me” while still wanting “to make a real deal” (https://x.com/i/status/2123831860299985193), and called the war “a little bit of a military move” (@ClashReport, https://x.com/i/status/2123831612563701852). Israeli Ambassador Yechiel Leiter said US troops come home “when Iran is defeated” (https://x.com/i/status/2123812362402632018).

**Why it matters for Europe.** Renewed escalation would hit Hormuz shipping and energy prices again, with a second tanker hit near Khasab this week.

**What to watch.** Official US statement on Qeshm; UKMTO updates; US force posture (F-35C departures vs reinforcement).

## Update: Saudi-led coalition strikes Hodeidah port, Sanaa and power plants; Houthis claim hits on Shuqaiq power station and Jizan port

****Summary:** The Saudi-led coalition said it began a “special operation” against Houthi targets, hitting capabilities at Hodeidah port it says were used to smuggle weapons and ballistic missiles, and ballistic-missile and drone sites in Sanaa (@sentdefender, https://x.com/i/status/2123783553720725783; Al Jazeera, https://x.com/i/status/2123777486571856035, https://x.com/i/status/2123783668917567504).**

Video showed black smoke over Hodeidah (https://x.com/i/status/2123786496301797507). Saudi Arabia also targeted the Saada and Mokha power stations (https://x.com/i/status/2123756693003624804). Early-warning alerts sounded in Jizan after missile launches from Yemen (https://x.com/i/status/2123761888446103792); a Saudi spokesperson said “shrapnel” fell in the Shuqaiq area, and the Houthis (Ansarallah) claimed they targeted the Al-Shuqaiq power station and the port of Jizan in retaliation (https://x.com/i/status/2123801618709663968, https://x.com/i/status/2123832101040472485).

**Why it matters for Europe.** A direct Saudi–Houthi war around Red Sea ports threatens the Suez route for European trade again; Houthi claims are claims.

**What to watch.** Damage at Shuqaiq/Jizan; Houthi threats to Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District; shipping advisories.

## Ukraine's strikes: Russian missile launchers hit; fuel-sale limits spread in Russian regions

****Summary:** Kyiv says Ukrainian forces struck three Russian ballistic missile launchers and an air-defence gun-missile system, destroying one launcher (Kyiv Independent, https://x.com/i/status/2123799788080525772).**

Reuters reports more Russian regions are limiting fuel sales as Ukrainian drone strikes cut refinery output (https://x.com/i/status/2123793544515125726), and that strikes on the Crimean bridge are straining Russian logistics (https://x.com/i/status/2123757030284382244). Defense News: Ukraine plans to double drone supplies and triple deep strikes with European partners' support, despite Washington's request to prioritise front-line defence (defensenews.com Europe).

**Why it matters for Europe.** Ukraine's deep-strike campaign rests increasingly on European support, an area where European decisions diverge from US preferences.

## Russia's RS-28 Sarmat ICBM may have entered service, IISS says

****Summary:** IISS analysis (Missile Dialogue Initiative, Matthew Bint) says satellite imagery suggests the nuclear-armed RS-28 Sarmat ICBM may have begun to enter service with the Strategic Rocket Forces, and further flight trials may take place as Moscow looks to ease reliability concerns (https://x.com/i/status/2123753731523018978).**

Background (CEAFE archive): @F_CEAFE has tracked Sarmat since December 2019 (plans for next-generation hypersonic warheads) and March 2020, when Russia completed the upgrade of its serial-production facilities (TASS).

**Why it matters for Europe.** The strategic balance moves while US–Russia “denuclearization” talks are discussed; Europe has no seat at that table beyond the French and British deterrents (see France–Sweden exercise, 08:30 edition).
