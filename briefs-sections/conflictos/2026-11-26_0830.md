## Update: Yemen war — Houthis claim strikes on Ras Tanura, Jeddah, Jizan and Riyadh

Ansarallah (the Houthis) claimed attacks on four targets inside Saudi Arabia on 25 November, including the Ras Tanura refinery and export terminal in the Eastern Province (Drop Site News via @MenchOsint, https://x.com/i/status/2125677126405746923; Antiwar.com relaying spokesman Yahya Saree, https://x.com/i/status/2125742753904509431). The group says it also targeted the ports of Jeddah and Jizan. The Saudi spokesperson acknowledged a hit at Jizan and debris falling at Jeddah (@MenchOsint, https://x.com/i/status/2125663583174602826, https://x.com/i/status/2125678483967971398; image: https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HX_tT-xXUAAkbqt.jpg). Overnight the coalition reported intercepting two ballistic missiles aimed at Riyadh and a cruise missile aimed at the west of the kingdom (Al Jazeera English, https://x.com/i/status/2125705039268221282, https://x.com/i/status/2125692731947094275). CGTN (Chinese state media) aired smoke over a Saudi refinery (https://x.com/i/status/2125816827850985478). The scale of damage at Ras Tanura is unconfirmed; one account on the list calls it a "major blow" to the world's largest offshore crude loading facility (https://x.com/i/status/2125687840059330723, commentary).

**Why it matters for Europe:** Ras Tanura is a key node for Saudi exports. A sustained Houthi campaign against Saudi energy, on top of the Hormuz mining, threatens oil and fuel prices in Europe.

## Update: Iran war — tanker hit off Dubai as Washington tightens "coordinated action" in Hormuz

After Iran said ships had hit mines in Hormuz and warned vessels to coordinate passage (Reuters, https://x.com/i/status/2125683477169201571), UKMTO reported another tanker struck by a projectile 50 nm west of Dubai (via @MenchOsint, https://x.com/i/status/2125733447713038447). US Under-Secretary of State Allison Hooker said she held "productive" talks with GCC leaders and Jordan and claimed coordinated action in the Strait is increasing pressure on Iran (Al Jazeera English, https://x.com/i/status/2125683925339209806). US envoy Jared Kushner said President Trump is keeping all options open on Iran and confirmed he has met Iranian officials (Al Jazeera English, https://x.com/i/status/2125756730600813014). Iranian officials' accounts relayed on the list reject US plans for a blockade (retweets of @araghchi and @ir_rezaee, https://x.com/i/status/2125656590699438102, https://x.com/i/status/2125656541336379481; Iranian government statements, reported as claims).

## Poland asks NATO to study shooting down Russian missiles and drones over Ukraine

Poland has formally asked NATO to explore intercepting Russian drones and missiles over Ukraine when they are heading towards Alliance territory, after three Russian Kh-101 cruise missiles struck Poland last week (@sentdefender, https://x.com/i/status/2125795946369806648; image: https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HYBYJYxXYAAJ9Zn.jpg; original source not cited, unverified).

**Why it matters for Europe:** this would push NATO air defence beyond its borders for the first time. It is the most direct escalation debate on the eastern flank.

## Ukraine war — ballistic strike on Kyiv; delays on US air-defence missiles

Russia launched a large ballistic missile attack on Ukraine late on 25 November, hitting a shopping centre in Kyiv and injuring three people (Kyiv Independent, https://x.com/i/status/2125707526897561629). Zelensky said there was "no good news" from the US about speeding up deliveries of air-defence missiles (Kyiv Independent, https://x.com/i/status/2125729185071268303, https://x.com/i/status/2125729242654912845). The US approved a possible Foreign Military Sale of F-16 sustainment for Ukraine worth an estimated $621.9 million (@Varsevan via @Schizointel, https://x.com/i/status/2125709796976312680; image: https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HX_jv0qXwAAQf1I.jpg). One tally puts US security assistance under the second Trump administration at $8.49bn in FMS, $10bn through PURL, $700m in USAI and $300m in PDA (@Schizointel, https://x.com/i/status/2125711592473940326, unverified). More than 30 countries called on Russia to stop using toxic chemicals and riot-control agents as weapons of war in Ukraine (Kyiv Independent, https://x.com/i/status/2125777287249383623).

## Russia's war machine: refineries at a 20-year low, mass cheap cruise missiles, barriers against sea drones

Rystad Energy says Russian refinery runs have fallen to 2.6–2.7 million barrels a day, the lowest in more than two decades, as Ukraine keeps striking refineries (quoted by @RALee85, https://x.com/i/status/2125704548589170872; chart: https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HYAE6ZlW8AALeYf.png). Rob Lee says Russia plans about 4,200 cheap cruise missiles in 2026 and aims for 30,000 next year (https://x.com/i/status/2125702447955898418). A Russian military channel reports that three Russian fleets now use floating barriers to keep Ukrainian sea drones out of their harbours (relayed by @Schizointel, https://x.com/i/status/2125730924084039714, unverified). Military Times reports estimates of more than 700,000 Russian soldiers killed, more than all US war deaths since the First World War (https://x.com/i/status/2125658763218620709, https://x.com/i/status/2125655876283351267).
