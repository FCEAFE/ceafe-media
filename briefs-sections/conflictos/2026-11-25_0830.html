Russia escalates pressure on the Baltic states: formal warning to Estonia, Latvia weighs border closure

Russia’s Foreign Ministry accused Estonia of hostile actions “on the verge of triggering open war”, linking it to Ukrainian drone strikes; Estonian Foreign Minister Margus Tsahkna dismissed the accusation as “Russian propaganda” (Reuters, https://x.com/i/status/2125409136120828129). Commentators describe it as Moscow’s first formal warning to a NATO member of this kind (https://x.com/i/status/2125347552404856860). Latvia’s Prime Minister Andris Kulbergs has asked ministries to assess restrictions on land crossings with Russia and present a position by next week, following Estonia’s closure (https://x.com/i/status/2125319296007967132). Separately, Zelensky confirmed that Ukraine was responsible for the attack on the Russian cargo ship Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland (@FaytuksNetwork, https://x.com/i/status/2125432139831463941).

  • Why it matters for Europe: Russia is building a pretext against an EU and NATO member on the Baltic, while the war’s maritime front reaches the Gulf of Finland, next to Finnish and Estonian waters.
  • What to watch: Latvian government position next week; any Russian follow-up measures against Estonia; Finnish reaction after Helsinki summoned the Russian ambassador (previous edition).

Ukraine’s deep strikes on Russian refineries and Russia’s strikes on Ukraine

A large Ukrainian drone raid hit Russia overnight on 25 November; monitoring channels report two oil refineries set ablaze, with Syzran and its refinery in the Volga basin among the targets (Kyiv Independent, https://x.com/i/status/2125396503925535133; @OSINTtechnical, https://x.com/i/status/2125358622288855548). A Russian attack on Odesa Oblast killed two people, including a teenager, and a strike on a Kyiv residential courtyard injured two (Kyiv Independent, https://x.com/i/status/2125369334335692849). A pro-Russian count claims at least 405 successful Russian strikes on Ukrainian railway infrastructure in 2026 (claim, https://x.com/i/status/2125446727893168631). The Russia-installed authorities in Donetsk claim a Ukrainian drone hit a bus station there (claim, https://x.com/i/status/2125439319967969667).

  • Why it matters for Europe: the energy war hits both sides’ winter resilience and keeps oil and gas prices high for Europe.
  • What to watch: confirmation of the refineries hit; Russian retaliation against Ukraine’s grid.

Update: Yemen–Saudi war — Hodeidah port struck after Houthi hit on Riyadh tower

The Saudi-led coalition ordered civilians to evacuate Hodeidah port; the Houthis warned they would target Jeddah and other Saudi ports in return (Al Jazeera, https://x.com/i/status/2125388005955514515). Houthi-run Saba then reported coalition strikes on Hodeidah port and Hodeidah airport (https://x.com/i/status/2125364389347999863, https://x.com/i/status/2125397445760929929); video shows fires in the port (https://x.com/i/status/2125409052356379113), and @visegrad24 reports further Saudi strikes on the port this morning (https://x.com/i/status/2125466236330869197). Saba also reports a Saudi strike on vehicles in Lahj governorate leaving 25 people dead or missing (claim, https://x.com/i/status/2125379651438842341). The WSJ reports the Houthis hit Riyadh’s Public Investment Fund tower for the second time in five days, injuring 11 (via https://x.com/i/status/2125426479609610484); India says two of its nationals were among the injured, and Qatar condemned the attack (Al Jazeera, https://x.com/i/status/2125470910975221926, https://x.com/i/status/2125341188391907612). The US Embassy in Jerusalem issued a region-wide alert over Houthi and Iranian threats (https://x.com/i/status/2125330492065288542).

  • Why it matters for Europe: a Saudi–Houthi war on the Red Sea coast threatens Suez traffic again and adds to the Hormuz disruption hitting European energy supply.
  • What to watch: Houthi strikes on Saudi Red Sea ports; status of Saudi airspace.

Iran war: funding, blockade and the Hormuz front

Trump told the New York Post that funding for US operations against Iran is secured through the end of January 2027 and warned Democrats not to turn a “total win” into a loss when they take control of Congress (Al Jazeera, https://x.com/i/status/2125412908083638363). A poll cited by Al Jazeera finds 55% of US voters want the war to end immediately, regardless of terms (https://x.com/i/status/2125439333532676209). Iran’s Supreme National Security Council secretary Mohsen Rezaei called US blockade plans “delusional and futile” (Iranian claim, https://x.com/i/status/2125351720951947472). Israeli Defence Minister Israel Katz says Iran is developing chemical weapons (claim, via https://x.com/i/status/2125322976988114972). @ClashReport shared footage reportedly showing a US/Israeli strike on an Iran Air Airbus at Bushehr at the start of the war (unverified, https://x.com/i/status/2125336301016584481).

  • Why it matters for Europe: a war funded at least into 2027 keeps Hormuz risk, energy prices and US munitions demand high, competing with European and Ukrainian needs.
  • What to watch: US confirmation or denial of the MQ-9 loss; blockade measures.

Sources: https://x.com/i/status/2125412908083638363, https://x.com/i/status/2125439333532676209, https://x.com/i/status/2125351720951947472, https://x.com/i/status/2125322976988114972, https://x.com/i/status/2125336301016584481

Ukraine war at sea off Libya: Haftar forces count losses, Italy alarmed

Haftar’s Libyan Arab Armed Forces announced that a navy officer and an NCO were killed in the Ukrainian attack on Benghazi port, and vowed revenge (https://x.com/i/status/2125348519754047918). The Financial Times count, relayed on the list, puts 16 major Russian naval vessels struck since 2022 (8 lost, 8 severely damaged) (https://x.com/i/status/2125351277727256977). Italy has voiced concern that the war is spreading into the Mediterranean (https://x.com/i/status/2125433342262644806).

  • Why it matters for Europe: Naval combat in the central Mediterranean brings the Ukraine war to the EU’s southern flank, near Italy and Malta.
  • What to watch: movements of the Russian convoy in the Sicily Channel (Early warning).

Sources: https://x.com/i/status/2125348519754047918, https://x.com/i/status/2125351277727256977, https://x.com/i/status/2125433342262644806

Counter-space and space: US proliferated military constellations; Shenzhou-24 docks

Jonathan McDowell lists the US military constellations now being deployed, including the Space Force’s SDN R communications and SDN TH radar satellites, the Space Development Agency’s missile trackers and the NRO’s proliferated architecture with at least two sub-series (https://x.com/i/status/2125382900220645768). China’s Shenzhou-24 crewed spacecraft launched and docked with the Tiangong station on 25 November, according to the China Manned Space Agency (state media, CGTN, https://x.com/i/status/2125441789608014257). No new satellite shadowing or proximity incident was reported in this window.

  • Why it matters for Europe: the US and China are scaling military space while Europe’s own constellations (IRIS²) are still years away.

Sources: https://x.com/i/status/2125382900220645768, https://x.com/i/status/2125441789608014257

Defence contracts with a war link

Boeing has a US Air Force contract for 15 more KC-46A tankers (203 on contract or in service worldwide) (@defpostmedia, https://x.com/i/status/2125460346253307915). Elbit Systems Deutschland will supply integrated naval electronic warfare suites for the German Navy’s F-128 frigates (https://x.com/i/status/2125469419241426958), and Thales will deliver STAR NG and RSM NG radars to the Royal Netherlands Air and Space Force (https://x.com/i/status/2125465960060698831).

  • Why it matters for Europe: an Israeli group’s German subsidiary wins EW on a German frigate, while a European supplier (Thales) equips a European air force.
