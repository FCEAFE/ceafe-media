## Update: Iran war — more tanker fires in Hormuz, bulk carrier hit off the UAE; Tehran orders a tighter blockade

Iran says two more oil tankers caught fire after striking naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz; Al Jazeera notes the claims have not been independently verified (https://x.com/i/status/2125161737288548511). The IRGC Navy speaks of a fourth “violating” tanker on fire (via https://x.com/i/status/2125186466922004933), and satellite fire detections support at least one ship fire overnight and another in the morning (satellite fire detections). UKMTO separately reports a bulk carrier hit by an unknown projectile at anchor 23 nautical miles north-east of Khor Fakkan (https://x.com/i/status/2125146633188806713). According to Iranian reports, the chief of the regular army (Artesh) ordered the navy commander to tighten the blockade of the strait “day by day” until Washington accepts Tehran’s terms (@IranObserver0, https://x.com/i/status/2125174346972889596). Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said a land blockade of Iran is “virtually impossible” given its size (Iranian state media via Al Jazeera, https://x.com/i/status/2125157475120759287). Despite this, oil fell to three-month lows as rising supply offsets the Hormuz threat (Reuters, https://x.com/i/status/2125191480377561326). On the cyber front, the US Coast Guard has deployed cyber teams after attacks on up to 50 energy tankers (@ClashReport, https://x.com/i/status/2125157229712060513).

- **Why it matters for Europe:** the threat now reaches ships waiting off the UAE, outside the strait itself, raising insurance and freight costs for Europe’s energy imports.
- **Follow-up:** UKMTO details on the Khor Fakkan vessel; names and flags of the burning tankers; any US response to the blockade order.

## Ukraine war: 355 drones toward Moscow; Russian strikes kill 9; Western parts in Russian drones

Moscow mayor Sergei Sobyanin said 355 drones flew towards the capital between 23:00 on 23 November and 08:00 on 24 November (relayed via https://x.com/i/status/2125172938185789689); Russia’s Defence Ministry claims 699 Ukrainian drones were shot down overnight over Russian regions and the Black Sea (claim relayed via https://x.com/i/status/2125126375313215925). Russian attacks killed 9 people and injured 77 across Ukraine over the past day; in Zaporizhzhia Oblast a five-storey residential building was partly destroyed, killing two and injuring 44 (Kyiv Independent, https://x.com/i/status/2125194936035238171), and Zelensky said Russian strikes focused on energy in the Zhytomyr region (reposted by @F_CEAFE). A Ukrainian intelligence report seen by the Kyiv Independent finds US and European components in a Geran-4 jet drone shot down in August; 12 parts carry dates, all made in 2024–2026 (https://x.com/i/status/2125130441258684535). @OSINTtechnical shows the R-280 highway, a key Russian supply route in the occupied south, littered with destroyed trucks (https://x.com/i/status/2125175908038254835). A Guardian analysis of leaked Rosstat records lists 1,087 Russian military suicides since the start of the Ukraine war (@ClashReport, https://x.com/i/status/2125171316638753135).

- **Why it matters for Europe:** European components still reach Russian drones despite sanctions, a sanctions-enforcement issue for the EU.
- **Follow-up:** damage assessment around Moscow; the new EU sanctions package (topic 6).

## North-eastern flank: Geran-5 in Poland, Russian build-up near Finland, Sarmat

The drone that entered Poland on the night of 23–24 November is now described by independent analysts as a jet-powered Geran-5 that reached about 25 km into Polish territory (Update; https://x.com/i/status/2125131447254106462). Yle reports Russia is modernising a base near the Finnish border for up to 11,000 troops (https://x.com/i/status/2125172958024835478), and Latvia is reportedly considering closing its border with Russia (unverified, https://x.com/i/status/2125125375529287688). IISS says the Sarmat heavy ICBM may have begun entering service (https://x.com/i/status/2125152951849070740). NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte called on member states to prepare society as a whole for the risk of war with Russia (relayed via https://x.com/i/status/2125184097974628762).

- **Why it matters for Europe:** jet drones are faster and harder to intercept than Shaheds; the Finnish-border build-up will define NATO’s newest front.
- **Follow-up:** Polish debris or radar data; Finnish Defence Forces comment on the Yle report.

## Update: Libya — Kyiv calls the Benghazi strikes self-defence; imagery confirms the *Angara*

Ukraine’s Foreign Ministry spokesman Heorhii Tykhyi said the strikes on Russian ships in Libya were lawful self-defence, arguing that “the threat to Libya was created precisely by Russian warships and Russian arms carriers” (Kyiv Independent, https://x.com/i/status/2125185563741295039). OSINT imagery confirms the cargo ship hit near Benghazi was the Ro-Ro *Angara* (via @FaytuksNetwork, https://x.com/i/status/2125166566157562165). Libyan authorities condemned the attacks (CGTN, state media, https://x.com/i/status/2125122246595207224). Unverified aggregator claims say an Iranian vessel, *Alvan*, was also hit by naval drones off Libya (https://x.com/i/status/2125127769466040334), and that Albania denied drones were launched from its territory (https://x.com/i/status/2125126659418554801).

- **Why it matters for Europe:** the naval war now touches the central Mediterranean, Libya’s politics and, through rumours, NATO members such as Albania.
- **Follow-up:** confirmation or denial of the *Alvan* report; Tripoli and LNA positions.

## Yemen–Saudi war: Houthis claim 11th Wing Loong-2; UK sanctions

Yemen’s Ansarallah (Houthis) say they shot down another Saudi-operated Wing Loong-2 drone over Lahj governorate, the eleventh since the escalation began (@MenchOsint, https://x.com/i/status/2125154806079594778). Pro-Houthi accounts claim damage at the Shuqaiq power station in Jizan after a Yemeni attack (via https://x.com/i/status/2125198550522806468). Both are unverified, partisan claims. The UK announced sanctions on the Houthis for undermining stability in the Middle East and on four senior officials over human rights abuses (via @FaytuksNetwork, https://x.com/i/status/2125193504695705936).

- **Follow-up:** Saudi confirmation; damage at Shuqaiq, a major power and desalination site.
