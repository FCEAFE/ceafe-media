Update: Italians killed in Kuwait — Iran denies, Riyadh condemns “Iranian aggression”, Araghchi calls Rome

Iran officially denied involvement in the drone strike in Kuwait that killed two Italian technicians. An Iranian military official called it a “false flag” (@IranObserver0 https://x.com/i/status/2126018640231215321). Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said he gave his condolences in calls with his Italian (Antonio Tajani) and Kuwaiti counterparts (@araghchi via @GeromanAT https://x.com/i/status/2126044199967670486). Pro-Iranian accounts call the incident a psyop to pull Europe into the war (https://x.com/i/status/2126089288437624937). Donald Trump: “Victory [in Iran] will happen whenever I want it to happen” (clip via @MenchOsint https://x.com/i/status/2126093293884707104, not checked against the original).

Why it matters for Europe: these are the first European deaths in the Gulf war, and Rome and Tehran are now talking directly. Responsibility is still disputed, and that will shape any Italian or EU response.

What to watch: Kuwaiti and Italian findings on the drone’s origin, and the security of European contractors at Gulf bases.

Update: Saudi–Yemen war — new launches, Houthi pressure on Aden

After the hit on the Jizan refinery, more launches from Yemen disrupted Riyadh airspace in the evening, and alerts sounded in Abha and Khamis Mushait (see Early warning). @MenchOsint notes that the Saudi spokesperson reported a drone intercepted about 30 minutes before the alerts were issued (https://x.com/i/status/2126079116634132728). Russian milblog Rybar and @war_cube report Ansar Allah (Houthi) forces advancing towards Aden and building defences near Aryab on the Abyan border (via @GeromanAT https://x.com/i/status/2126048132773339148, https://x.com/i/status/2126044156518715395; unverified). A Sana’a-based journalist says Ansar Allah sees Saudi Arabia as “the head of the snake” (@DropSiteNews via @MenchOsint https://x.com/i/status/2126149359368601947).

Why it matters for Europe: Saudi energy infrastructure (Ras Tanura, Jizan) is directly at stake for oil prices and the Red Sea route. A Houthi push on Aden would widen the war on land.

What to watch: confirmed damage at Ras Tanura, Saudi strikes in response, and the front in Abyan.

Ukraine war: night strikes; Berlin and Rome urge Kyiv not to widen the war

Footage on Russian channels shows a jet-powered Geran-5 drone over Kharkiv, where an aviation institute was hit, and a drone strike in Odesa. Both cities report deaths and damage over two days (@Zlatti_71 https://x.com/i/status/2126131729714852176). Kryvyi Rih has been under waves of drone strikes since 25 November, with power cuts (https://x.com/i/status/2126124406015476017). A Russian strike on the evening of 26 November set an unnamed enterprise in Khmelnytskyi district on fire; there were no casualties (https://x.com/i/status/2126127873152975235). Germany and Italy both cautioned Kyiv against carrying the war into new regions after Ukrainian drone strikes on Russian vessels in the Baltic and off Libya (https://x.com/i/status/2126125122645831813). Libya’s LNA reportedly threatened Ukraine with retaliation over the drone attack in Benghazi port (repost via @GeromanAT https://x.com/i/status/2126050847045955924; unverified).

Why it matters for Europe: Ukraine’s maritime strikes in the Baltic and the Mediterranean bring the war onto Europe’s doorstep. Berlin and Rome now publicly want to contain it.

What to watch: Russian naval escort patterns in the Mediterranean, and Kyiv’s reply to its European partners.

Washington’s Ukraine line: Trump says Putin wants a settlement; Kushner speaks of a “European proxy war”

Trump said Vladimir Putin wants the conflict settled and that all sides must want it. The remarks follow a joint statement with NATO’s Secretary General and a Kremlin reply (@Zlatti_71 https://x.com/i/status/2126133080775708914). Trump also said he told Putin, during their talks in China, that he wants him to “pull back all military aggression” (@FaytuksNetwork https://x.com/i/status/2126049981530313094). Jared Kushner called the war a “European proxy war”: “Europe is funding and arming Ukraine against Russia” (clip via @MichaKobs https://x.com/i/status/2126130131525661051). He also said no pressure has been put on Ukraine and that Washington wants to give it formal security guarantees (@mtracey https://x.com/i/status/2126182371506401282). The quotes come from clips and were not checked against full originals.

Why it matters for Europe: the US negotiators describe Europe as a party to the war, not as a party at the table. No single EU state can change that alone; only a common European position can.

What to watch: what the security guarantees contain, and whether Europeans get a seat at the next round.

Space and strategic forces: nuclear-satellite denial, “Starmind” and China–Russia talks

Moscow denies any plan for a nuclear-armed spacecraft (see Early warning). Chinese state broadcaster CGTN reports that the US government approved a SpaceX constellation of up to one million satellites (“Starmind”) for AI data centres in orbit (https://x.com/i/status/2126154042288087362). CGTN also reports the Chinese and Russian militaries held a strategic consultation by video conference on 26 November (https://x.com/i/status/2126143975384113271) and promotes China–Pakistan cooperation in orbit (https://x.com/i/status/2126121326075072856). These are state-media claims. In the United States, the Army’s first Dark Eagle (LRHW) hypersonic battery is fully fielded and operational, nearly three years late, according to Lt. Gen. Lozano (@FaytuksNetwork https://x.com/i/status/2126067091086413966; image: https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HYFOwGWW4AA8NYb.jpg).

Why it matters for Europe: the denial follows a German report on a nuclear-armed Russian satellite (covered on 24 November). Mega-constellations under US licence deepen Europe’s dependence in orbit.

What to watch: any Russian launch matching the reported profile, and EU and ESA responses on sovereign constellations.

Northern Europe: Sweden’s NATO contribution bill; seizures in the Baltic

A government bill now before the Swedish Riksdag (2026/27:6, Sweden’s contribution to NATO deterrence and defence in 2027) would let the government deploy up to 3,900 ground troops and 850 naval personnel, among other forces, without a new vote in parliament. This is the reading of a critical commentator, @mazzenilsson (https://x.com/i/status/2126085054224892270); the bill text was not checked. A video commentary relayed by @GeromanAT says the Wall Street Journal reported Nordic alarm after Russia’s Baltic Fleet ended two years of NATO-member ship seizures (https://x.com/i/status/2126043386419269963; WSJ original not checked). Nordic Monitor reports calls to remove Türkiye’s ambassador to Finland over a fund scandal (https://x.com/i/status/2126148927766364573).

Why it matters for Europe: standing Nordic contributions to NATO’s forward posture are the backbone of Baltic deterrence.

What to watch: the Riksdag debate and Russian naval escorts in the Baltic.
